# A same-direction mutation battery pins a two-sided bound from one side only

## Context

Plan `test-suite-anti-vacuity` (PR #1443, merged `33c8140f3`) existed to remove
assertions that pass under the defect they name. Its own defect archetype then
recurred *inside its own fixes* across three independent chains — a
`regex_overfit` / not-bound-to-its-scenario class that four pre-submission
self-review rounds and ten CodeRabbit comments each caught one narrowing later
than the last.

The chain that makes the mechanism legible is the heading-region one. Finding
`85a302` widened `_HEADING_RE` to accept CommonMark's 0-3 leading spaces and
added a four-space control. Finding `1fc7e3` then found the sibling
`_NEXT_STEP_HEADING = re.compile(r'\n {0,3}###(?:[ \t]+|.)')` with a battery of
**four ADMIT arms** (two-space, three-space, tab-after-marker, bare-marker) plus
one REJECT arm on the *marker* dimension — and nothing at all on the *indent*
dimension's upper edge. The finding proved the consequence by probe rather than
by argument: widening the quantifier to `{0,4}` or to any-indent left **all four
loop arms green, the level-4 subsection control green, and the live-document
assertion green**. The bound could move to any value with zero red anywhere in
the module.

The same shape produced the `_ARCHIVED_WRITE_PROHIBITION` chain (sentence
co-occurrence → destination preposition → target noun) and the
`_partition_divergence` one, where an aggregate-count check let a dropped
occurrence and a double-assigned occurrence cancel to an equal total.

## Root cause

A mutation arm proves a bound only on the side it exercises. A battery whose
arms all sit **inside** the accepted region demonstrates that the region is at
least that wide; it says nothing about whether the region is *too* wide, because
widening the bound cannot redden an arm that was already inside it. The arms
therefore look like thorough coverage — four of them, parametrized, all failing
under the pre-fix pattern — while the constraint they appear to pin is free to
move outward unobserved.

The discipline that caught the recurrences is the same discipline that admitted
them: each fix was mutation-proven, and each mutation exercised the direction the
fix had just narrowed. A fix credited as structurally immune on the strength of a
one-directional proof is credited beyond its evidence, and the next occurrence
enters through the unpinned side.

## Proposed action

When a guard's property is a **bound** rather than a membership test, require the
mutation battery to bracket it from both sides before the fix is considered
proven:

- At least one arm that must go **red when the bound is widened** (an input just
  outside the legal region that the guard must reject).
- At least one arm that must go **red when the bound is narrowed** (an input just
  inside it that the guard must accept).
- State in the fix record which direction each arm pins. A record listing only
  admitting arms is a one-sided proof and must say so rather than reading as
  complete.

For a partition or accounting check, the equivalent is: compare **occurrence
identities as a multiset**, not aggregate totals, and report missing and
duplicated separately — aggregate equality lets two opposite errors cancel.

Do not resolve a finding of this class by weakening the comment to match the
looser code: that leaves the guard loose and re-seeds the class.

## Impact

Applies to every population-derived, regex-derived, or partition-derived guard in
the corpus. The plan that discovered it shipped fixes for five occurrences and a
sixth was still found by review afterwards, so the archetype survives a
single-plan sweep.
