# On doc-claim plans prefer narrowing over rewording; self-review self-seeds

On a documentation-claims plan, pre-submission self-review ran ~7 rounds: each narrowing correction exposed the next nearby over-claim, while rounds that reworded a claim produced new findings. Every fix made by rewording creates new text that can be reviewed, so the loop does not settle by itself — and the loop-back ceiling (max_iterations=3, twice exceeded by operator authorization here) turns into a routine prompt instead of a backstop.

## Solution

When a self-review finding is an over-claim, prefer deleting or narrowing it to a statement checkable against code, rather than rewording it. After N rounds whose findings all come from text the plan itself wrote, report the self-seeding pattern to the operator early; consider a separate convergence rule for doc-claim plans instead of re-running full rounds.

## Impact

Fewer loop-back rounds on doc-heavy plans; the ceiling stays a backstop.
