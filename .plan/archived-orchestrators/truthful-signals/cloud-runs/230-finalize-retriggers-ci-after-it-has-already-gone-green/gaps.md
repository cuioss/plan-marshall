Remove `lessons-capture` either way, and do **not** substitute `finalize-step-lessons-housekeeping` or `finalize-step-sync-baseline` — both are ordered below the build and would be a fresh wrong example.
- **Done when:** `push.md` names no step whose authoritative doc declares `mutates_source: false`, names no step ordered at or below `default:pre-push-quality-gate`'s `order`, and every step it does name re-derives from its own frontmatter against those two conditions.
