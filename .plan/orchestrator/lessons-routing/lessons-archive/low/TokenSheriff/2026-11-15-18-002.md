# Scope a finalize re-review to the delta since the last reviewed HEAD when a fix commit is narrow and disjoint

Scope a finalize re-review to the delta since the last reviewed HEAD when the new commits are a narrow fix whose footprint is disjoint from the reviewed production surface, and carry the earlier closing verdict forward, instead of re-running the full delta-plus-closing envelope pair on every fix commit.

## What happened

In plan client-refresh-verification-discovery-cache, `default:functional-review` and `default:code-review` each ran several times in one 6-finalize run: the initial pass, the pass after the code-review loop-back fix (cdc31c), and again after the TASK-011 loop-back from the CodeRabbit fix (finalize re-entered from the start at HEAD 36b61921). Each execution dispatched two envelopes (a delta round and a closing round). Counting the work-log DISPATCH rows, functional-review dispatched 7 times and code-review 6 times. The rounds were re-run four times in total because each fix commit moved HEAD and invalidated the prior review's HEAD binding.

## Cost

- Each re-run costs a level-3 envelope pair of roughly 1.5 to 6 minutes, plus the pre-push quality gate re-running a whole-tree verify of about 30 minutes.
- The later re-reviews over a one-file test fix (TASK-011 touched only `TokenSheriffClientDiscoveryCacheTest.java`) found nothing new.

## Rule

- When the new commits are test-only, or touch a single file already reviewed, review the delta since the last reviewed HEAD and carry the earlier closing verdict forward for the unchanged remainder.
- Keep the full re-run only when the fix touches production files outside the previously reviewed delta.
