## Problem

Two calls, one unchanged tree, minutes apart: first `stale` (ledger scan, narrow
build rows), then `fresh` (build-decision short-circuit before any scan) after a
covering verify. Both cannot be right; the dangerous shape is the second — a permit
without scanning reports `fresh` exactly like a ledger-corroborated pass (caller on
`status` alone cannot tell affirmative verification from vacuous permit).

## Directive

Reconcile the routes (authoritative when they disagree); distinguish short-circuit
`fresh` from corroborated `fresh` (ADR-009); regression test pinning both routes to one
fixture.
