# A merge hold written into the epic ledger does not reach a plan that is already running

Observed by the orchestrator in the analyze of PLAN-30 (epic `kidicap-gateway-requirements`, PR #448, merge commit b7b98699), 2026-10-14.

## What happened

While PLAN-30 was in its CI phase, an orchestrator session saw `Http2EarlyResponseResetIT` time out in attempts 1 and 2 of the PR's Integration Tests run (passing on attempt 3) and recorded a top-priority Open Defect in `epic.md`: "#448 must not merge until fixed and green over several consecutive runs". It landed that ledger change as #453. The running plan never read it. It merged #448 through the merge queue about an hour later on a green merge-group run and filed the hang as follow-up issue #455, with the cause stated as not established.

The plan did nothing wrong by its own contract: its spec carried no such hold, and a plan reads its spec at init, not the epic ledger. The orchestrator's hold was written where only the orchestrator reads.

## Rule

A hold on a plan that is already running cannot be placed in the ledger. The ledger is read by the orchestrator only; a launched plan reads its spec once, at init. To stop a running plan from merging, the instruction has to reach the plan's own channel: tell the operator so they can intervene in the running session, or put it on the PR, where the plan's review and CI steps read. A merge-blocking mechanism (for example a draft status or a required check) would also work. Record the hold in the ledger as well, but do not rely on it there.

Applies to `plan-marshall:plan-orchestrator` (the analyze and status verbs and any mid-flight finding).
