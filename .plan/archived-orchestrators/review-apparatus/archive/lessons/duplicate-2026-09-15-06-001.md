## Context

Round 1 of `pre-submission-self-review` filed a blocking `contract_drift` finding asserting that a new
Step 3b verifier was structurally unreachable, reasoning from `dispatch-inline-split.md` line 19
classifying `default:pre-submission-self-review` under \"Dispatched steps\" and therefore concluding the
WHOLE step body runs inside a leaf. The premise was false: the same document places Step 1 and Step 4 of
the same step inline in the dispatcher context, so Step 3b is reachable from there too. Eight further
loop-back rounds were spent before the contradiction was adjudicated and the finding rejected (resolution:
`rejected`, premise refuted in round 2, rounds 3-10 ran the verifier successfully — the same shape recurred
identically as a second finding, also rejected on the same refuted premise).

## Root cause

A roster label is a coarse, step-level classification. It says nothing about the placement of any
particular sub-step of that step's own body — some sub-steps of a \"dispatched\" step may run inline in the
dispatcher context before or after the dispatch, exactly as documented elsewhere in the same file. A
self-review pass that reads only the roster label, without cross-checking the same document's own
per-step placement statements, manufactures a structural-impossibility finding from a classification that
was never that granular.

## Proposed action

Before filing a finding that a dispatch is structurally impossible on the strength of a roster
classification, read the SAME document's own per-sub-step placement statements. The empirical check is
cheap: a context that already issues one dispatch can issue a second — reachability from the dispatcher
context is not exclusive to one dispatch per step.

## Evidence

- Q-Gate finding `20faec` (and its recurrence, `d5a5ff`-shaped duplicate) — both resolution `rejected`
- `dispatch-inline-split.md` line 19 — the roster classification that was over-read
- The same document's own placement of Step 1 and Step 4 inline in the dispatcher context, which the
  finding never consulted
- Cost: 8 loop-back rounds spent adjudicating a refuted premise before the correct reading held"
