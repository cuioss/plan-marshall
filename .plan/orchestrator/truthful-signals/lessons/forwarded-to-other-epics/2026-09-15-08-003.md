# Split a 12-deliverable, 44-task multi_module plan before executing it

## Context

This plan consumed 10,488,689 tokens against the `multi_module + bug_fix` error anchor of 2.0M — 5.2x over — and 380 minutes of worked time against a 150-minute error anchor. It carried 12 deliverables and 44 tasks. Phase 5-execute alone accounted for 6,330,694 tokens (60 percent of the plan) across 18 dispatches and 3 phase re-entries; 6-finalize added a further 2,412,805 across 12 dispatches with 4 loop-back iterations.

## Root cause

Nothing in the plan phase caps deliverable or task count against the scope/change-type anchor the retrospective later scores the plan against. A 12-deliverable bug-fix plan is scored by an anchor calibrated on plans an order of magnitude smaller, and the mismatch is only discovered after the spend.

## Solution

Teach phase-4-plan to read the same `(scope_estimate, change_type)` anchor table the retrospective uses, and when the composed task set projects past the warning column, surface a split proposal to the operator before execute begins. The anchor table already exists at `plan-retrospective/references/plan-efficiency.md` Section 2 — this is a read of an existing calibration, not a new one.

## Impact

The over-spend is not a one-off: deliverable 5 alone declared 19 files across 8 skills, which is a plan-sized unit inside a deliverable. Splitting at plan time is cheap; discovering the mismatch at retrospective time is not.
