# files_exist Q-Gate mechanical check assumes host-worktree-relative step targets, false-positiving on external-repo (org_checkout) plans

## Context

Plan `org-empty-review-guard-too-broad` has its entire footprint in another repository (`cuioss/cuioss-organization`, referred to as `{org_checkout}`). Its deliverables carry an explicit `**Repository:**` field naming the target tree, and `references.json:affected_files` is deliberately left empty because every consumer of that field resolves paths against the *host* worktree (see `solution_outline.md` § "Execution and Landing Model" § "Why references.json:affected_files is intentionally left empty").

At Step 8, `manage-tasks qgate-mechanical-checks` ran its `files_exist` check against the four created tasks' `steps[].target` paths. The check resolves every target against the host worktree disk regardless of the deliverable's declared `**Repository:**` field, so it flagged 4 of the 10 step targets as missing — every `read`-intent step (TASK-003's three reads, TASK-004's one read) — even though every one of those paths exists and is correct **inside `{org_checkout}`**. The 2 `write-replace` and 1 `write-new` steps (TASK-001, TASK-002) were not flagged, so the check's predicate appears intent-asymmetric on top of the repo-scope gap.

## Resolution approach

For this plan, the findings are dispositioned `taken_into_account` at the Step 1 re-entry: each deliverable's `**Repository:**` field and the outline's Execution and Landing Model section are the authoritative evidence that the paths are correct relative to `{org_checkout}`, not the host worktree — the mechanical check has no way to consult that field, so a human/LLM re-entry pass is required to close the loop for any plan of this shape.

There is no general fix recommended here (out of this plan's scope to implement); this lesson exists so the next external-repo-footprint plan recognizes the `files_exist` false-positive as expected rather than re-diagnosing it from scratch. A future improvement to `qgate-mechanical-checks` could special-case a `**Repository:**`-bearing deliverable by skipping the host-disk existence check (or resolving against a declared external root), rather than resolving all step targets against the host `.plan/`-adjacent worktree unconditionally.
