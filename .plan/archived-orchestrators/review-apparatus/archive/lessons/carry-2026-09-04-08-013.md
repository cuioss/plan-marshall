## Root cause

The recurrence is the finding. 5835d8 is an asserted count-and-kind claim about a population, written in the same commit that fixed three other asserted claims, by an agent that had just articulated the archetype in writing. Awareness of the archetype at the moment of authorship did not prevent it.

The revealing detail is the split: 5835d8's own `cohort_size` was DERIVED — re-scanning all 79 surfaced user-facing strings across the 23-file surface — while the prose inside the artifact was asserted. The discipline was applied to the metric and not to the narrative, in the same document.

## Proposed action

Treat a count-or-kind claim in prose as a claim owing the same derivation the surrounding metrics already receive: any sentence naming a number of sites, a kind of site, or an absence must cite the enumeration that produced it, or be deleted.

Both instances were resolved by deletion, and in 5835d8's case the repository's current-state-only documentation standard already disfavoured the paragraph on independent grounds. That suggests a cheaper structural guard than a semantic one: reject historical narrative in a test docstring outright, removing the site where this class keeps landing.
