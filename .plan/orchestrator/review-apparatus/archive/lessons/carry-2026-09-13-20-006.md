title: "Rewriting a guard's claim to match its narrower reality is a deferral, not a resolution - it hands the close to the reviewer"
content: "## The rule

When a self-review finds that a guard's **claimed population is not the population it actually sweeps**, rewriting the claim to match the narrower reality is **not a resolution — it is a deferral**.

The honest-docstring remedy is correct only when closing the gap is genuinely out of the plan's scope AND nothing downstream depends on the guard's coverage.

**The test to apply**: if the remedy is *\"document the gap\"*, ask **who closes it, and when**. If the answer is *\"a reviewer, on this same PR\"*, close it now — ⛔ a review round is far more expensive than the fix, especially under a one-review-per-hour rate window.

## The instance: the same gap billed two review rounds

- Self-review Q-Gate finding `f0869c` (6-finalize, round 8): the marker-shape sweep claimed to be *\"population-derived over the live registry\"* while its SHAPE axis was a hardcoded triple. The plan chose the documenting remedy — the docstring was rewritten to *name* the fail-open blind spot and name the accessor that closing it would require. The gap was recorded as known and left open.
- CodeRabbit `0f4db5` (PR #1477 round 1, Minor): the BOT axis of that same sweep is a hardcoded tuple.
- CodeRabbit `d9e200` (round 2, Minor): the SHAPE axis — explicitly *\"the residual half of a finding already accepted earlier in this PR\"*. The bot population had been derived in TASK-012, *\"but the `(issue_comment, review_body, inline)` tuple still bounds WHAT is swept, so a marker declared on any other publish shape passes unseen and the test title claim quietly stops holding.\"*

⭐ **Both CodeRabbit comments cite this project's OWN corpus learning back at it**: *\"For every set-guarding detector, derive the population from its authoritative source at build time or runtime.\"* The knowledge was present, recorded, and not applied.

## The compounding shape

The plan's own self-review found it FIRST. The deferral is what handed it to the reviewer — twice, on two axes, across two rate-limited rounds. A gap documented in a docstring reads to every later reader as an accepted design.
