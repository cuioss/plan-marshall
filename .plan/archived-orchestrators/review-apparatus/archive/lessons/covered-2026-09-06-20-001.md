title: "display_detail on the automatic-review step record is byte-identical across rounds, so it carries no progress"
content: "## Problem

`display_detail` on the automatic-review step record came back byte-identical across two consecutive re-review rounds (both \"4 comment(s) found - 1 reviewed, 1 empty, 1 refused\"). Only `head_at_completion` distinguishes them.

A reader diffing step records on detail text would see no change across a genuinely new round, so the field cannot carry progress on its own.

## Directive

Establish whether `display_detail` is relied on anywhere as a progress signal across rounds. Where it is, either make the rendered text vary with the round's own observations or route the progress question to `head_at_completion`, which does distinguish the rounds.

## Evidence

PR #1427, rounds 2 and 3 of the automatic-review step record."
