# Two script calls in the finalize CI check were rejected for their arguments

## What happened

Plan plan-25-integration-test-evidence (PR #408), 2026-10-08T18:14Z, during the `ci-verify` step of a late finalize round. Two calls were rejected by the argument parser (exit code 2) within four seconds; the step was completed afterwards.

1. `plan-marshall:phase-6-finalize:ci_verify run` - rejected, required flag `--worktree-path` missing.
2. `plan-marshall:tools-integration-ci:ci --plan-id plan-25-integration-test-evidence pr comments --pr-number 408 --unresolved-only` - rejected as written.

## Observations for the pickup

- Call 2 puts `--plan-id` before the verb, the position documented for the CI verbs that declare no `--plan-id` of their own. It was still rejected for `pr comments`. The project's CLAUDE.md documents the same check as `ci pr comments --pr-number <n> --unresolved-only` with no `--plan-id` at all. Which position `pr comments` requires was not established in this run; the three written forms do not agree.
- Both rejections happened in a round the orchestrator drove by hand after the loop-back ceiling was spent, i.e. the calls were composed from memory rather than from the step's documented invocation.

## Rule

Take the argument form of a script call from the script's own help output or its canonical-invocations section, per verb. The position of `--plan-id` on the CI script differs between verbs and cannot be inferred from a neighbouring verb.

- Work log entries 15c7fc and 255ed9 (both `script_failure`, `failure_kind=argparse_rejection`), followed by cdb431 (ci-verify done).

## Correction (added at promotion, 2026-10-12)

The orchestrator re-ran both forms on 2026-10-12: `ci --plan-id X pr comments --pr-number N --unresolved-only` (flag before the verb) is accepted, `ci pr comments --plan-id X ...` is rejected with a note saying the flag belongs before the verb, and the form with no `--plan-id` is accepted too. The three written forms therefore do agree; the rule below stands.
