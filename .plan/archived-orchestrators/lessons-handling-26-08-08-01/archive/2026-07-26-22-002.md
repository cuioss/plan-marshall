# merge-window-accounting scans the global logs for a [LOCK] marker that is never emitted there, so its zero-contention report is unmeasured rather than healthy

## Observed

Surfaced by the full-corpus archived-plan retrospective audit (115 plans, 2026-07-26).

- The `merge-window-accounting` check buckets global `[LOCK] (merge:*)` lifecycle lines by `lock_id`
  and reported `plans_with_merge_events: 0`, `contended_plans: 0`, `total_blocked: 0`,
  `max_waiting_observed: 0`, with an EMPTY rows table (`rows[0]`).
- The marker does not exist in the store the check reads. A marker histogram over
  `.plan/local/logs/work-*.log` yields only `[INFO] [STATUS] [DISPATCH] [ARTIFACT] [ATTEMPT]
  [WARNING] [ERROR] [STEWARD]` — **zero `[LOCK]` lines of any kind**, merge or build-queue.
- Yet the corpus demonstrably merged 115 plans through the queue-enforced merge path
  (`branch-cleanup` display details record "merged via queue"), and the widened merge mutex plus
  FIFO admission queue (#849) is live.

## Impact

The check cannot distinguish "no contention occurred" from "contention is not observable from this
store". It renders the former. That is a **false-healthy signal** on exactly the accounting surface
the widened mutex was introduced to make visible: the merge-window cost the #849 trade buys is
currently unmeasured, and the `merge_window_ci_rerun` cross-check coupling is floored to
`informational` by the same blindness (`contended_plans=0` while `ci_rerun_plans=51`).

## How to apply

- Determine where the merge-mutex/FIFO lifecycle is actually recorded (the main-checkout lock store
  vs the global log) and point the check at that source, or emit `[LOCK]` lifecycle lines into the
  global log so the documented scan has something to find.
- Until then the check must report `unmeasured`, not `0` — an empty rows table under a never-emitted
  marker is absence of data, not absence of contention (the same no-false-healthy floor
  `input-integrity` enforces for metrics-blind plans).
- General rule: a detector keyed on a marker string must have a test asserting the producer emits that
  marker; otherwise the detector degrades silently to a permanent all-clear.
