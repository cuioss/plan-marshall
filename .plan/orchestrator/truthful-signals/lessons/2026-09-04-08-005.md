# Reject a fix-task whose files fall outside its deliverable declared surface

**Carried out of the `review-apparatus` epic inbox** — message `review-packs-become-published-artifacts-003.md`, sender `review-packs-become-published-artifacts`, promoted by the orchestrator drain of 2026-09-04.

## Context

The plan shipped 23 files. All 11 declared modification-intent paths landed, so every deliverable reads as 100% fulfilled and `check-artifact-consistency` reports `affected_files_recall: 100%`. The coverage metrics are all green.

The other 12 files — 52% of the landed footprint — sit outside every deliverable's declared surface. They are not scattered drift but two coherent, unrelated subsystems:

- build-server explicit-timeout routing: `build-server-client/SKILL.md`, `build_server.py`, `marshalld.py`, `_build_cli.py`, `_build_execute_factory.py`, `_build_server_protocol.py`, `pyproject.toml`, and 2 build-server tests
- the plugin-doctor analyze-step configurable contract: 3 plugin-doctor tests

A PR titled `feat(pr-agent): publish review packs to pr-agent-settings` landed a build-server protocol change.

## Root cause

Tasks 8 ("Reconcile the --timeout surface — validate the input, correct the ceiling-vs-floor prose") and 10 ("Separate two direct submits that differ only in explicit --timeout") are attributed to **deliverable 1**, whose declared surface is `marketplace/targets/pr_agent/target.py`, `marketplace/targets/generate.py` and three of its tests. Their actual footprint is the build-server subsystem, which deliverable 1 never declared.

Deliverable attribution on a task added during execute is not checked against that deliverable's declared paths. Because the recall metric is computed over the DECLARED set, adding out-of-surface work to a deliverable cannot lower any coverage figure — it is invisible to every existing check. The 5-file scope-creep bar in request-result-alignment is the only detector, and it fires after the fact in the retrospective, long after the PR has merged.

## Proposed action

When a task is added or re-targeted during execute, compare its declared step targets against the declared file surface of the deliverable it names. On a mismatch, require either an explicit deliverable-surface amendment or attribution to a new deliverable — rather than silently accepting the attribution.

Surface the running scope-creep count during execute, not only in the retrospective, so the divergence is visible while the branch can still be split.

## Evidence

- aspect: request_result_alignment — 12 scope-creep files, 2.4x the 5-file acceptability bar, 52% of the landed footprint
- aspect: outline_vs_shipped — `touched_but_unassessed: 12 of 23 realized_footprint_paths`, listing exactly the build-server and plugin-doctor clusters
- aspect: artifact_consistency — `affected_files_recall: 100%` and `affected_files_exact_match: warn` with `references_only[20]`; recall being computed over the declared set is why the creep is invisible to it
- `manage-tasks list` — tasks 8 and 10, domain `plan-marshall-plugin-dev`, both attributed to deliverable 1
