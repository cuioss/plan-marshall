## Rule

Retrospective callers must query `--help` for accepted flags before passing
`--output-file` (undeclared flags fail the pipeline at the parser),
resolve the plan directory exactly once without doubling the path, and treat
missing fragment files as a structured skip rather than an exit-1 chain.
A `done` outcome over advisory output can mask all three at once.
