# Offer an operator close when self-review findings turn self-seeded

## Context

In plan `opencode-bootstrap-executor-fix` the `pre-submission-self-review` step fired 12 times (10 `loop_back`, 1 earlier `done`, 1 final operator close). `loop_back_iteration` reached 5, and the dispatcher refused iteration 6 ("Loop-back ceiling breached — requested iteration 6 against a ceiling of 5"). The yield was uneven. Loop-back rounds 0–3 returned 0–1 findings, or a verdict the verifier refused on a hand-count mismatch (525 vs 499). Round 4 found a real code defect: `PM_MARKETPLACE_ROOT` was never demoted on any generator verb. Round 5 returned 8 findings. The round-5 author reported that 4 of its 7 `contract_drift` findings were on prose the round-4 fix had just written. The operator then directed one last fix round, followed by push, PR and review handling. That skipped the closing self-review round and `pre-push-quality-gate` on HEAD 534dce96f.

## Root cause

The loop has no exit other than a clean round or the ceiling. A round can report how many of its findings are self-seeded, but that signal never shortens the loop or offers the operator a decision. So once the real defects are gone, each fix writes new prose, the next round flags that prose, and the loop runs until the ceiling stops it.

## Proposed action

- In the self-review dispatcher (`phase-6-finalize` pre-submission-self-review), count each round's findings that sit on lines the previous loop-back fix wrote.
- When most of a round's findings are self-seeded and the round found no behaviour-level defect, offer the operator an explicit close. Present it as "fix by deletion, then close", with the self-seeded share as the evidence.
- Record the close as `may_close: operator_override`, the same fact this plan recorded by hand.
- Keep the whole-tree verify gate. The canonical verify on 534dce96f (28407 tests) was still the evidence that allowed the push.

### Root cause

Unchanged, with two details this run adds:

- The self-seeded signal exists as a log line and is not wired to the operator offer, so the offer came only when the orchestrator judged the loop unproductive.
- A clean round does not end the loop when the verifier refuses the verdict on carried candidates (round 3). That is a second non-exit, separate from self-seeding.
