# Finding: merge-mutex hold spanned operator waits without release

## What happened

During `phase-6-finalize` of plan `issue-1697`, the cross-plan merge
mutex was acquired at the first `branch-cleanup` entry and held across
subsequent operator-wait boundaries (posture/review/CI-gate prompts)
without the release + FIFO-re-enqueue + re-acquire cycle that
`merge_hold_window == full_window_release_at_waits` invariant 1
requires. Recorded as a WARNING decision at hold time.

## Why it matters

A held lock across a human wait blocks every other plan's merge for the
duration. No contention occurred here (`waiting_count=0` throughout),
so this instance caused no harm — but the invariant exists precisely
for the contended case, and an uncontended pass is not evidence of
compliance.

## What is owed (process-compliance)

- The dispatcher should release + re-enqueue before every
  operator-wait boundary after the merge lock is acquired, and
  re-acquire + re-validate on resume — including waits owned by
  triage/CI-gate prompts, not only the merge-gate prompts.
