# A fix that establishes a rule is not done until the document is swept for every other site subject to that rule

## Recurrence — 2026-09-05 (shipped-guards-assume-the-meta-projects-own-layout)

The plan that filed this lesson is also its most complete demonstration: **six recurrences
inside one plan, three of them inside the fix for the one before.**

The run named them itself, in order, in its own decision log:

- entry 313 — `pre-submission-self-review` ROUND LOOP CLOSED OUT OF BUDGET, NOT CONVERGED.
  12 rounds ran (2-13), 26 findings filed and all fixed. The last clean full-surface pass was
  round 4. **Rounds 11, 12 and 13 each found a defect introduced by the IMMEDIATELY PRECEDING
  round's fix.**
- entry 343 — "FOURTH RECURRENCE of the plan's own archetype, named explicitly." HEAD
  `7f55d4549` was the fix for the previous round's three findings, and two of the next

- entry 358 — "SIXTH instance". The display_detail ceiling test budgets both single-name

The same shape appears one level up, in the **deliverable set** rather than in a document:
deliverable 3, titled "Dispose of **every** residual shape-B site the partition names",
declared 9 paths under `Files expected to mutate:` and shipped 6. One of the three misses,
`marshall-steward/references/upgrade-flow.md`, required an ad-hoc follow-up commit
(`b217b8493`) immediately after the PR merged.

**The operational lesson from this run is about termination, not about diligence.** The
round loop did not converge and could not have: entry 313 records that "only deletion
converges, and several of these findings required a corrected statement rather than a
removal". A correction is a new statement, and a new statement is a new site the sweep rule
applies to. So a review loop over doc-prose precision has no fixed point, and running it to
convergence is not a terminating strategy. The remedy is a sweep performed ONCE, over a
population derived at fix time from the rule the fix establishes — not iterated rounds each
of which re-seeds the population it is trying to exhaust.

Cost: `pre-submission-self-review` fired 15 times, 11 of them error-terminated, for at least
3.3M tokens — roughly a third of the 9,644,036-token plan.
