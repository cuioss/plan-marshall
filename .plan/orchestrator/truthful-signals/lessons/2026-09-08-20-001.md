# A mutates_source: false step that participates in the 6-finalize inline-replay loop-back tier leaves uncommittable edits

## Problem

`default:pre-submission-self-review` declares `mutates_source: false` (`workflow/pre-submission-self-review.md:8`) and its own body is find-only — it files findings and takes no FIX decision.

But the loop-back contract gives it a tier that mutates source. `SKILL.md` § "Loop-back Target Contract" defines `loop_back_target: 6-finalize` as **inline replay** for *"inline-fixable dispositions (SUPPRESS, narrow-rationale ACCEPT, single-annotation FIX with no fix-task allocation)"*. A single-annotation FIX is a source edit. So when this step loops back on that tier and the orchestrator adjudicates its findings as FIX, the step applies edits — and then:

- **item 5f does not commit them.** The instrumentation is gated on the step's declared `mutates_source`, which is `false`, so the dispatcher runs no porcelain check and cuts no commit.
- **the item-5f(0) post-run-band guard does not catch them either.** That guard fires only for a step declaring `post_run_review: true`; this step is `order: 7`, pre-merge, so it is outside the guard's scope.
- **`default:push` (order 11) then asserts a clean tree** and the run halts on a dirty worktree with no owner for the commit.

Observed on plan `pre-push-gate-mutation-coherence`, HEAD `2626d43ec`: self-review rounds 2 and 3 left five modified files with no committing owner. The orchestrator committed them by hand (`426982695`) to let the run proceed.

## Why it matters

This is the **same defect archetype the observing plan was created to fix**, one step over: a `mutates_source` declaration that is false about the step's actual behaviour. The plan flipped `default:pre-push-quality-gate` from `false` to `true` for exactly this reason — the step could leave edits the dispatcher would never commit.

The gap is structural, not incidental. Any find-only step reachable on the `6-finalize` inline-replay tier has it.

## Directive

Settle **who owns the commit** for a find-only step's loop-back fixes. Three candidate resolutions, to be decided on the merits rather than picked:

1. **Declare `mutates_source: true` on `pre-submission-self-review`.** Honest about the tier, and item 5f then commits. Cost: the step is find-only on its ordinary (non-loop-back) path, so the declaration would over-state the common case — though item 5f's porcelain check is whole-tree, so on a clean pass it is a no-op anyway. This is the cheapest fix and the one most consistent with what the observing plan just established for the sibling gate.
2. **Make the inline-replay tier's committing owner explicit in `SKILL.md` § "Loop-back Target Contract".** State that the tier's fixes are committed by the dispatcher regardless of the step's `mutates_source`, since the tier is what makes the step mutate. This keeps the declaration honest about the ordinary path.
3. **Forbid source edits on the tier for a `mutates_source: false` step** — route every FIX to `loop_back_target: 5-execute` (fix tasks) instead. Most conservative, but it defeats the tier's purpose, which exists precisely to avoid a full-phase rollback for a one-line correction.

Whichever is chosen, add a regression test asserting that a `mutates_source: false` step which leaves a dirty tree on the inline-replay tier is either committed or refused — never silently carried into the push barrier.

## Recurrence — 2026-09-08 (pre-push-gate-mutation-coherence, same run, post-merge retrospective)

Not a new occurrence — the same incident, re-examined after the plan landed. The
retrospective found **two further consequences** of the recorded `head_at_completion`
that the original filing does not name, and both outlive the dirty-tree halt.

### The anchor names a tree that lacks the step's own output

`pre-submission-self-review` recorded `outcome: done` with
`head_at_completion: 2626d43ecc283e211e3d55240a42a7650050e3da` and
`display_detail: "self-review clean: 31 candidates examined, no check matched"` at
19:58:44Z. The five files it had edited were committed by hand at 20:00Z as
`426982695`. So the SHA the step recorded as the tree it certified **does not
contain the step's own fixes**. The record is not merely incomplete; it is a
positive claim about the wrong tree.

Two mechanisms read that anchor and both are corrupted by it:

1. **Next-round delta scope.** `manage-status/SKILL.md` states the anchor is passed
   to the surfacer as `--since-ref` so a re-firing step scopes its next round's
   delta against it. A `--since-ref` that predates the previous round's own fixes
   re-surfaces work already done and cannot see whether the fixes introduced
   anything.
2. **The gate/review-delta instrument.** `review_gate_delta` requires
   `gate_head_sha == reviewed_head_sha`. `pre-push-quality-gate` also recorded
   `2626d43ec`, review happened at `426982695` and `e488a7f8a`, so the PR was
   excluded with `exclusion_reason: gate_tree_unsubstantiated` and
   `structural_share` withheld. The hand commit is one of the two hops that moved
   HEAD out from under the certified tree.

### Why the retrospective raises this here rather than filing separately

Same root cause, same step, same run: the step edited files it was not permitted to
commit, so every record it wrote about "the tree" is about a tree missing its work.
Any of the three candidate resolutions in the Directive above fixes the anchor too —
if item 5f commits the edits, the re-stamp path (`head_dependent: true`) then records
the post-commit SHA. Resolution 3 (route FIX to `5-execute`) fixes it differently, by
ensuring the step never has uncommitted output to be wrong about.
