# A rename reached the SKILL.md contract source and missed one site in the docstring that argued for it

Source: Q-Gate finding 0c2a6f (6-finalize, self-review; fixed in cc4e8cf1b).
Defect class contract_drift — 2 findings in this class this round.

manage-execution-manifest.py's reconcile docstring declares at line 3030 that the
partition is keyed on RESOLVABILITY, not loadability, and argues at 3031-3041 that
partitioning on loadability made the external half of the verb vacuous. Its stale and
broken bullets at 3021 and 3024 both say "unresolvable". The fail-closed paragraph at
lines 3052-3055 still said "every unloadable step is classified broken" — the pre-rename
word, contradicting the same docstring 24 lines above.

Its own contract source, manage-execution-manifest/SKILL.md line 405, carries the
corrected "every unresolvable step", which proves the rename was intended and this one
site was missed.

## Solution

Leaving it standing invites re-introducing the loadability partition the docstring itself
calls vacuous. A vocabulary rename is not done when the contract source is updated; the
implementation's own prose is part of the same set, and the one site a reader most
trusts is the one nearest the code.

## Impact

Self-contradicting within a single docstring, 24 lines apart.
