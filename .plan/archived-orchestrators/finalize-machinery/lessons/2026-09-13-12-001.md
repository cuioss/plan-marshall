## Context

PR #1480 fully green yet enqueued twice, sat through two 30-min windows with nothing
merging: the enqueue call returns success on the branch's queue rule existing, not on the
PR having joined. Automation could not distinguish waiting from never-entered; burned an
hour before a human noticed. Ledger read as though the queue path worked unaided (manual
recovery invisible to it).

## Proposed action

Positively verify PR membership post-enqueue (query the entry, confirm resolution);
treat "not in queue" as terminal (re-enqueue/escalate), never as slow path; carry what
was actually verified in the return.
