# Verify a defect claim handed to the retrospective before reporting it as a finding

## Provenance — this is a RESTORATION, not a new observation

Originally filed as **`2026-08-27-16-003`** (plan `a-failing-ci-call-reports-success`, PR #1356) and
retired on 2026-09-17 during a concurrent two-session sweep of the corpus, with a `completely_covered`

⛔ **The retirement was sound in bookkeeping and wrong in kind.** The sweep moved post-run-quality DEFECT
REPORTS into the `post-run-quality` epic, where a work item belongs. This entry is not a defect report: it

owned by `post-run-quality` PLAN-PRQ-09 D5. This entry governs the behaviour in the meantime and after.

## The rule

**Treat a defect claim supplied in a dispatch context as a HYPOTHESIS with a named verification, never as
a finding.** A claim arriving through the dispatch context carries the authority of the run that produced
it, and a reader who treats its inputs as facts will restate it — laundering an unverified assertion into
a report that then reads as corroborated *because of where it came from*.

Where the claim is a set property over a store you can read, **derive it**: list the population, read each
member, report the derived count.

## The original observation, verbatim

The dispatch context for one retrospective asserted a live defect: that the lessons corpus was filing
title-only stubs with empty bodies — *"5 active lessons, every one body-less, set-body never called after
add"*.

A census refuted it. `manage-lessons list --status active` returned **6** lessons, and reading every one
with `manage-lessons get` found every one carrying a full multi-section body (typically 1.5–2.5 KB). Zero
were body-less. The count was also stale by one: `2026-08-27-14-001` was filed after the dispatch context
was composed.

The claim was wrong in both its universal (*"every one"*) and its count (*"5"*), and was cheap to check —
one list call and six gets. Had it been restated, it would have filed a fabricated bug against
`manage-lessons` that read as corroborated.

## Evidence

- `manage-lessons list --status active` → total 6, filtered 6
- `manage-lessons get` on `2026-08-27-07-001`, `-07-002`, `-09-001`, `-12-001`, `-12-002`, `-14-001` → 6 of
  6 with full bodies
- Related standing rule: derive completeness, never assert it
