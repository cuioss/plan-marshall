# ci_complete_precondition resolve invoked without its required --pr-number and --worktree-path

## What happened

Plan `register-corpus-group-contamination`, phase 6-finalize, step `plan-marshall:automatic-review`
(2026-10-17T13:58:40Z): the call

`plan-marshall:phase-6-finalize:ci_complete_precondition resolve`

was issued without its two required flags and was rejected by argparse (exit 2, `failure_kind=argparse_rejection`):
"Add the required flag(s) ... ['pr-number', 'worktree-path']". The step retried and completed `done`, so the cost
was one failed call and a script-failure cluster that triggered the lessons-capture signal gate.

## Rule

`ci_complete_precondition resolve` takes `--pr-number` and `--worktree-path` as required arguments; it does not
derive either from `--plan-id` or from the plan status. Pass both, taken from the PR created at `create-pr`
(`pr_number`) and the plan's `worktree_path` metadata, on the first call. Quote the flag set from the script's
canonical-invocation block or its `--help`, never from the surrounding workflow prose.

## Possible improvement

If the automatic-review workflow doc shows the invocation without these flags, or with only `--plan-id`, its
example should be corrected to the full required flag set; alternatively the script could resolve both from the
plan status when `--plan-id` is given.
