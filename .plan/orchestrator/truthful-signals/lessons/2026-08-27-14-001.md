# ci pr create takes a verb-scoped plan-id while the router strips a plan-id anywhere in the argv

## Problem

The ci router consumes `--plan-id` as a **router-level** flag by scanning the entire argument vector and stripping it. But `pr create` **also** declares a **verb-scoped** `--plan-id`, used to resolve the prepared body store.

Passing `--plan-id` once on `pr create` is therefore ambiguous: the router may strip the copy the verb needs for the body store.

## Consequence

The documented *preferred* routing form (`--plan-id`) is unusable on this verb, so callers must fall back to `--project-dir`, which the SKILL.md presents as an escape hatch rather than the norm. A caller following the stated preference could get a body-store resolution failure with no obvious cause.

Observed 2026-08-27 during PLAN-TRUTH-114 finalize: the create-pr step routed both `ci` calls with `--project-dir <worktree>` specifically to avoid the double-`--plan-id` ambiguity, and flagged it rather than silently absorbing it.

## Directive

Establish the actual strip behaviour before relying on either form — do not assume:

- If the router strips only the **first** occurrence, or strips **positionally**, pin that with a test so a refactor cannot silently change it.
- If it strips **all** occurrences, `pr create` cannot be routed by `--plan-id` at all, and `tools-integration-ci/SKILL.md` must say so on that verb rather than presenting `--plan-id` as preferred everywhere.

## Archetype

A flag name reused at two scopes in one argv, where the outer consumer is greedy. The general shape is worth checking on any other ci verb that declares a flag the router also claims.
