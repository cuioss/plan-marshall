# Q-gate outline gaps and refine scope tension were stale but lesson-bearing

Source: plan-01-script-surface-validation (tooling-truthfulness), PR #1466.

## Problem

12 Q-gate findings across 2-refine (4), 3-outline (7: missing CERTAIN_INCLUDE
assessments for 6 files plus an architecture-mismatch on design notes), and
5-execute (1, fixed via TASK-004). Refine carried a broad `scope_estimate` over a
1-module mapping plus a 100-percent confidence flag. All were accepted as
stale/superseded by the phase-4 gate and execution.

## Guidance

Early-phase flags that resolve by execution still carry the slipped-then-caught
shape worth recording: a stale finding is not a false finding. When triaging
refine/outline Q-gate output, separate "superseded by later evidence" (record the
shape, move on) from "refuted" (re-scope) — collapsing the former into the latter
hides how often early phases over-flag.
