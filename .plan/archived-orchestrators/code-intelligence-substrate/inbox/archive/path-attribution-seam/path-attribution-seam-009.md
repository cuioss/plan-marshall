# The `[DISPATCH]` emission contract is honoured on first entry only — every loop-back and re-fire envelope is invisible, so the under-count grows with the cost of the plan

## What happened

`ref-workflow-architecture/standards/dispatch-logging.md` obliges every dispatch site to emit a `[DISPATCH]` work-log line, and the retrospective's execution-context dispatch audit is its enforcement consumer. On PLAN-CIS-023, `work.log` carries **18** `[DISPATCH]` lines. At least **22** envelopes actually ran.

The four (or more) envelopes that ran without a line, each proven by its own `[SKILL] (plan-marshall:execution-context.{name})` load lines and a matching `[STATUS] ... Complete`:

| Envelope | Ran at | Completed | Entry kind |
|----------|--------|-----------|------------|
| `phase-3-outline` | 12:36:09Z | 13:04:29Z | Q-Gate remediation re-entry |
| `phase-5-execute` | 17:22:50Z | 17:47:00Z | loop-back re-entry 1 (TASK-012) |
| `automatic-review` | 18:17:48Z | 18:24:25Z | step re-fire after loop-back |
| `phase-5-execute` | 18:57:58Z | 19:18:32Z | loop-back re-entry 2 (TASK-013) |

`analyze-logs` corroborates independently from a different signal, for phase 5 only:

```text
dispatch_clustering:
  inferred_dispatches: 4
  starting_markers: 0
  re_entering_markers: 4
```

Four inferred phase-5 dispatches; only two of them carry a `[DISPATCH]` line.

## The signature

**100% of the missing lines are re-entries. 0% are first entries.** Every first dispatch of a phase or a finalize step logged correctly; every loop-back re-entry, Q-Gate remediation re-entry, and post-loop-back step re-fire did not. This is not random loss — it is a whole code path (the re-entry path) that never picked up the emission.

## Why the direction of the bias is the problem

The under-count is **not uniform across plans — it is proportional to how much a plan struggled**. A plan that runs straight through logs every envelope. A plan that loops back twice, as this one did, loses exactly its two most expensive envelopes: both loop-back re-entries re-ran the full 5-execute verification chain (quality-gate + module-tests + orchestrator-tier verify and coverage) for a single fix task each.

So any consumer that counts envelopes, attributes cost per dispatch, or measures dispatch discipline will read loop-back-heavy plans as *cheaper and better behaved* than clean ones. The measurement is biased in favour of the runs that need scrutiny most.

There is a second-order effect on the audit itself. This aspect's inverse-coverage half flags "a step marked done with zero matching `[DISPATCH]` evidence" as inline-where-dispatch-was-required. On the current emission behaviour that check will fire on every re-fired step — a false positive generated by the very gap it is meant to detect.

## The rules

**Do X — emit `[DISPATCH]` from the dispatch primitive, not from each caller's first-entry branch.** A contract satisfied by convention at N call sites is satisfied at the N−k sites someone remembered; a contract satisfied by construction at the one place the dispatch actually happens has no re-entry path to miss.

**Do X — when auditing an emission contract, count the population from an INDEPENDENT signal and compare.** The gap here was found by pairing `[SKILL] execution-context.{name}` lines against `[DISPATCH]` lines — two signals of the same event. Counting only the `[DISPATCH]` lines would have reported 18 dispatches with no way to know 18 was wrong.

**Not Y — do not treat a re-entry as "the same dispatch continuing".** It is a new envelope with its own context load, its own token spend, and its own termination. Modelling it as a continuation of the first is what makes skipping the line feel correct.

**Not Y — do not validate an emission contract on a happy-path plan.** A straight-through plan has zero re-entries, so it satisfies the contract vacuously and proves nothing about the path that is broken.

## Detection

Deterministic and already half-built: `analyze-logs` computes `dispatch_clustering` for phase 5. Generalise it across all phases, pair each inferred dispatch (or each `[SKILL] (plan-marshall:execution-context.{name})` cluster) against the `[DISPATCH]` lines, and emit one finding per unpaired cluster. That turns a hand pairing into a fact the retrospective reports without an LLM in the loop.
