# Candidate lesson: a deliverable instructed a completeness claim be updated where no claim existed

- source_signal: qgate / 5-execute (D1 gate verdict)
- record_id: 2377a9
- component: plan-marshall:ref-workflow-architecture
- file: marketplace/bundles/plan-marshall/skills/ref-workflow-architecture/standards/dispatch-logging.md

## What happened

Two halves of the deliverable were premised on states the checkout did not exhibit. (a) The "forbidden hand-written emission" survived at ZERO dispatch sites — all eight hits of the literal were the standards that NAME the forbidden shape plus the tests that GUARD it. (b) `dispatch-logging.md` made NO completeness claim to update; its closing paragraph states a caller obligation with no roster, no count and no "every site" assertion. The instruction "update it where the rollout changes what it can honestly claim about completeness" had no claim to act on.

## Candidate rule

When a deliverable says "update the completeness claim", verify the claim EXISTS before editing. Manufacturing a completeness claim so the instruction has something to act on creates exactly the vacuous-authority artefact the work was meant to remove. Record "no claim exists" as a complete outcome.
