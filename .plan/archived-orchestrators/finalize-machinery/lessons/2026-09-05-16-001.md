# 2026-09-05-16-001 — reduced_transcript is truncated and reordered by the consumer TOON round-trip while its counts still describe the intact text

component: plan-marshall:plan-retrospective
category: bug
status: moved-to-epic (OPEN — staged in PLAN-05; corpus original retained until that plan ships)
source: corpus lesson 2026-09-05-16-001, copied into finalize-machinery per operator direction

## Context

`extract-chat-signal` invokes the platform-runtime op in a subprocess hop, parses stdout with
`parse_toon`, re-emits. The runtime's multi-line quoted `reduced_transcript` scalar does not
survive: 2,475 bytes/4 turns → ~575 bytes/2 turns (session ceab9df4); 4,641 bytes/16 turns →
~800 bytes/2 turns (e716a770); survivors reordered. Counts (`reduced_bytes`,
`operator_turn_count`, `gate_decision_count`) forward verbatim, describing intact text no
longer held. Pre-pass routes to Tier 1 ("feed transcript to LLM") on a fifth of the
transcript presented as complete.

## Root cause

Payload lost in transport (multi-line TOON scalar vs parse_toon) + counts not degrading with
payload. Distinct from 2026-09-04-14-002 (keep predicate); the reducer behaved correctly here.

## Proposed action

Stop round-tripping the payload through TOON (file path / single-line-escaped / base64);
re-measure `reduced_bytes` on the held value and publish the upstream figure separately;
route transport loss to Tier 2 with its own skip-reason token. Recurrence 2026-09-14 names the
mechanism: unescaped newlines re-parsed as record boundaries — escape at the producer.

## Impact

Every retrospective run on a transcript target reads ~a fifth of the operator interaction
while reporting full coverage; all behavioural verdicts computed over a truncated corpus.

## Epic status

Staged in PLAN-05 (lessons-pipeline, deliverable 4). Corpus original RETAINED until PLAN-05 ships.
