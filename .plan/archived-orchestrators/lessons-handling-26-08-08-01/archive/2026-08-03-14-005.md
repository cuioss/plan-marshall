# sonar-roundtrip pruned as no_code_delta while compose logged the footprint unresolvable

## Context

Two decision-log entries written by the manifest composer in the same second, at `2026-08-02T20:18:20Z`:

```text
[c11f4a] [STATUS] pre_push_quality_gate_inactive — kept pre-push-quality-gate on an unknown build
         verdict: plan footprint unresolvable — worktree not yet materialised
[9e1b0f] [STATUS] lane_resolution — dropped sonar-roundtrip from phase_6.steps
         (execution_profile=standard): effective tier full exceeds the standard posture cutoff
```

The composer states plainly that it cannot resolve the plan's footprint, then drops `sonar-roundtrip`, whose prune predicate is `no_code_delta` — a predicate *about the footprint*.

Re-evaluated against the realized footprint, `check-routing-decisions` returns:

```text
mis_prune:sonar-roundtrip,fail,no_code_delta,predicate_evaluated,
  sonar-roundtrip skipped as no_code_delta but the realized footprint touched production code
```

The plan modified four production Python files — `architecture.py`, `_cmd_client.py`, `_cmd_client_handlers.py`, `claude_pretooluse_hook.py` — plus three test modules. Sonar never analysed any of them.

## Root cause

Lane resolution runs at manifest-compose time (phase 4), before phase 5 materialises the worktree. A footprint-keyed predicate evaluated before the footprint exists is not evaluating the predicate; it is evaluating a default. The composer already knows this — it says so for `pre-push-quality-gate` in the adjacent entry — and handles the two steps inconsistently: `pre-push-quality-gate` is **kept** on the unknown verdict (fail-safe), while `sonar-roundtrip` is **dropped** (fail-open).

Two mechanisms are entangled in one decision. The `sonar-roundtrip` drop is attributed to the posture cutoff (`effective tier full exceeds the standard posture cutoff`), which is a legitimate, footprint-independent reason. But the step's `prunable_when` is `no_code_delta`, so the retrospective's re-evaluation correctly scores it against the footprint and fails it. Whichever mechanism actually fired, the recorded rationale and the recorded predicate disagree — and the outcome was fail-open on a code-bearing change.

## Proposed action

1. Apply the `pre_push_quality_gate_inactive` treatment uniformly: when the footprint is unresolvable at compose time, a footprint-keyed prune predicate must resolve to **keep**, never to drop. Fail-safe on unknown is already the composer's own established behaviour for one step; make it the rule.
2. Separate posture-cutoff drops from predicate drops in the decision log. A step dropped for posture should not carry a footprint predicate in its `prunable_when` at re-evaluation time, or the retrospective's mis-prune check will score it against evidence that never governed it.
3. Re-resolve footprint-keyed lane decisions at phase-5 exit, when the footprint is real, rather than committing them at compose time. This is the same recompose seam `execution.toon` already supports.
4. Add a compose-time test: unresolvable footprint + a step whose `prunable_when` is footprint-keyed ⇒ step retained.

## Consequence on this plan

`sonar-roundtrip` was the only static-analysis pass with visibility into new-code issues on the four production files. Its absence was not detected by any gate; it surfaced only in this retrospective, after merge. Two independent guard bypasses in `claude_pretooluse_hook.py` shipped to the PR and were caught by review bots rather than by tooling — not necessarily ones Sonar would have found, but the plan ran with one fewer net than its posture implied.
