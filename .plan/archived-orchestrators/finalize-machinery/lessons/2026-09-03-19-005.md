## Context

`always-on-is-not-a-resolve` (single_module/bug_fix, 5 files, 3 tasks) recorded
3.53M tokens (2.7x the error anchor); `6-finalize` alone 2.06M (58%). Four distinct
`head_at_completion` values inside finalize — HEAD moved four times, each move
invalidating already-passed head-bound steps. The quality gate ran 4x for 3 tasks
(12 step firings beyond first-fire).

## Root cause

Head-bound steps invalidate on ANY HEAD movement, including movement finalize itself
caused. The predicate is HEAD identity, not footprint overlap.

## Proposed action

Narrow the invalidation predicate to "HEAD changed in a way this step's inputs can
see" (realized diff vs declared surface; era-stamp-fill's `verdict_inputs` preserved
is the working existence proof), or order committing steps ahead of validating steps.

## Evidence

- plan_efficiency ([BUDGET] error tripped), logging_gap_analysis, invariant_summary
- Recurrence plan-145 (PR #1406): 18 refires of 34 firings, 2.35M tokens (66%)
  bought no new work; 3 of 5 loop-backs were quota waits advancing only the pointer.

## Coverage (finalize-machinery)

Remedy shipped by PLAN-01 / PR #1505: single-pass HEAD anchor (gate's
`head_at_completion`); no settle step advances HEAD past the certified tree; bound
≤1 gate firing per pass plus one per genuine loop-back. Corpus original retired as
completely_covered.
