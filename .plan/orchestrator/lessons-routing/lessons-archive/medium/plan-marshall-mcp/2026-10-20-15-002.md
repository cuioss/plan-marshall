# adr-propose Step 5 prompts the operator through AskUserQuestion although the step runs as a dispatched leaf

## Defect

`phase-6-finalize` `workflow/adr-propose.md` Step 5 asks the operator about each proposal through `AskUserQuestion`. The step runs as a dispatched `execution-context` leaf, which cannot fire `AskUserQuestion` (see `ref-workflow-architecture/standards/agents.md`, the section on leaves and AskUserQuestion). The step that was written cannot run in the place where it runs.

## Observed

Plan `question-corpus-public-datasets-2`: the leaf had to work around the step by returning the proposals in its result so that the main context could ask the operator. The workflow document defines no prompt-required envelope for that handoff.

## Corrective action

Rewrite Step 5 to follow the contract for leaves: return a prompt-required envelope that lists the proposals, and let the main-context dispatcher ask the operator and record the answers. Or move the operator prompt into the dispatcher in `phase-6-finalize/SKILL.md`. A plugin-doctor rule that flags `AskUserQuestion` in any workflow document dispatched as a leaf would catch this class of defect.
