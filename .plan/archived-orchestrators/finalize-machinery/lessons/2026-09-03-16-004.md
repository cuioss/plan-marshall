## Observation

PR #1387: 6 bot comments promoted, 3 substantive, all declined — but bucketed
inconsistently (a refuting rationale filed as `accepted`, corrected post-hoc to
`rejected`; "Declined —" rationale on `accepted`; design-decision decline on
`taken_into_account`).

## Recommended rule

Rationale refutes the claim → `rejected` (feeds false_positives_count); acknowledges
but defers → `taken_into_account`/`accepted`. Check at triage time (rationale opening
with refutation language belongs on `rejected`), not at retrospective.
