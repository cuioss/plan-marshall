# Re-close the 5-execute metrics row when a finalize loop-back re-enters it

## Context

This plan looped back from `6-finalize` into `5-execute` twice — at 21:12:55Z and 22:16:02Z, each recorded by `manage-status` as `Loop-back set-phase 6-finalize -> 5-execute` — and ran further execute work both times. Its `work/metrics.toon` `[5-execute]` row nevertheless still reads `close_count: 1`, `value_scope: single_close`, and `end_time: 2026-09-08T16:00:43Z`, the timestamp of the FIRST close. `generate` consequently reported `re_entered_phases[0]` — an empty list — on a plan carrying `metadata.loop_back_iteration: 3`.

The token consequence is exact and checkable. The phase row's accumulator-fed `total_tokens` is 4,884,953; its `dispatch_boundary_total` is 5,314,683. The 429,730-token difference is precisely the sum of the two dispatch-boundary rows timestamped AFTER the row's `end_time` (316,867 at 21:34:38Z plus 112,863 at 22:23:04Z) — the loop-back dispatches the phase row never absorbed. The wall and idle spans for both loop-back windows are missing from the row entirely.

## Root cause

The loop-back continuation path calls `manage-status set-phase` to move the plan back to `5-execute` but calls no `manage-metrics` boundary verb. `manage-metrics` already implements accumulate-on-re-entry correctly — `end-phase` adds the new active span, increments `close_count`, and stamps `value_scope: mixed_cumulative_and_last_close` — so the machinery is present and simply never invoked on this path. The phase-transition boundary is stamped once, at the original forward transition, and a loop-back re-entry is invisible to it.

The defect survived because the reconciliation rule masked its most visible symptom: `generate` picks the largest eligible measure, so `dispatch_boundary_total` won and the rendered token figure was right. The safety net worked; the row it was compensating for stayed wrong.

## Proposed action

On the loop-back continuation path in `phase-6-finalize`, pair the `set-phase 6-finalize -> {target}` call with `manage-metrics phase-boundary --prev-phase 6-finalize --next-phase {target}`, and pair the return transition with its mirror. The two calls are one indivisible action, exactly as `[DISPATCH]` and its dispatch are — a `set-phase` without the boundary stamp silently drops a whole re-entry window's attribution.

As a structural backstop, extend `manage-metrics reconcile-ledgers` with an arm that reports any dispatch-boundary row whose timestamp falls outside its phase row's `start_time..end_time` window. That is a two-field comparison over data already persisted, and it converts this class from something found by hand-differencing two published totals into a published finding.

## Impact

`re_entered_phases` is the authoritative re-entry signal — it is derived from `close_count`, which is written at the write site. A plan that looped back three times reporting an empty `re_entered_phases` is a false-clean on the one field a consumer is told to trust. Any cross-plan analysis that keys on it under-counts loop-backs, and any per-phase wall/idle roll-up under-counts re-entered phases by the whole duration of every re-entry window.
