# Q-Gate auto-fix loop burns an iteration re-dispatching phase-4-plan for informational triage findings the re-entry guard can't act on

During phase-4-plan of the `harden-claude-runtime-fail-safes` plan, the Q-Gate
validation pass produced two findings and the orchestrator's auto-fix loop
re-dispatched phase-4-plan to "fix" them — but the re-dispatch was a structural
no-op that resolved nothing and only burned an auto-fix iteration and a full
phase-envelope spawn.

## Observed sequence

```
[STATUS] (plan-marshall:phase-4-plan) qgate_validation_required=true
[DISPATCH] role=q-gate-validation ...
(plan-marshall:plan-marshall:qgate) Auto-fix iteration 1/3: 2 findings — re-dispatching phase-4-plan
[SKILL]   (plan-marshall:execution-context.phase-4-plan) Loaded plan-marshall:phase-4-plan
[STATUS]  (plan-marshall:phase-4-plan) Starting plan phase
[ERROR]   (plan-marshall:phase-4-plan) Re-entry guard: 3 task(s) already exist —
          refusing to re-create tasks (would duplicate the queue). Returning tasks_already_exist.
```

The two findings were both `triage`-type, `source=qgate`, informational flags:

1. `Q-Gate: Missing CERTAIN_INCLUDE assessments for D1 affected files` — an
   informational coverage flag; on a single_module bug_fix plan with an explicit
   Root Cause section the assessment step was reasonably skipped at outline.
2. `Q-Gate: Profile overlap — test_permission_ops.py in both D1 and D2` — an
   intentional-by-design overlap (D1 unit tests vs D2 cross-cutting regression
   tests in distinct test classes against the same file).

Both were ultimately resolved `taken_into_account` by **triage judgment** — NOT
by anything the re-dispatched phase-4-plan did. The re-dispatch could not have
helped: (a) phase-4-plan's re-entry guard refuses to re-create the task queue
once tasks exist, so it returns `tasks_already_exist` immediately; and (b) a
`triage`-type informational finding requiring a human/agent judgment call is not
the kind of finding a re-run of the planning phase can mechanically "fix" anyway.

## Why it matters

The Q-Gate auto-fix loop treats every outstanding finding as fixable-by-phase-
re-dispatch and decrements its `iteration N/3` budget accordingly. When the only
outstanding findings are `triage`-type informational flags (the dominant Q-Gate
finding shape at phase-4-plan for well-scoped plans), the re-dispatch:

- spawns a full phase-4-plan execution-context envelope (token + latency cost)
  that immediately bounces off the re-entry guard with `tasks_already_exist`;
- consumes one of the three auto-fix iterations without resolving anything;
- leaves the findings to be resolved by the triage path that would have run
  regardless.

The cost was harmless here (the plan proceeded), but it is pure waste that
recurs on every well-scoped single_module plan whose Q-Gate emits informational
triage flags — exactly the common case.

## Suggested remediation (improvement)

The auto-fix loop should distinguish findings a phase re-dispatch can mechanically
act on from `triage`-type informational findings that only triage judgment can
resolve, and NOT spend an auto-fix iteration re-dispatching the producing phase
for the latter. Concretely, before counting a finding toward the auto-fix budget
and re-dispatching:

- Skip `type=triage` findings that are already resolved (or that are pure
  informational flags with `source=qgate`) — route them to the triage/resolve
  path instead of a phase re-dispatch.
- For phase-4-plan specifically, recognize that the re-entry guard makes a
  task-recreation re-dispatch a guaranteed no-op once tasks exist; a Q-Gate
  finding that does not require re-deriving the task queue should never trigger
  a phase-4-plan re-dispatch.

This keeps the auto-fix iteration budget for findings that a re-run can actually
remediate (e.g. genuine module-mapping or scope-criterion defects that would
change task derivation) and avoids the wasted envelope spawn for the common
informational-triage case.

## Impact

Affects the Q-Gate auto-fix loop in `plan-marshall:plan-marshall` on every plan
that reaches phase-4-plan with outstanding informational `triage` findings —
i.e. the typical well-scoped single_module plan. Each such plan currently pays
one wasted phase-4-plan envelope spawn and one consumed auto-fix iteration per
Q-Gate triage finding batch.
