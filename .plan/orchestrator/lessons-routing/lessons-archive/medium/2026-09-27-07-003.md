# phase-3-outline leaf self-transitions 3-outline->4-plan before the orchestrator's q-gate-validation and Step-3 user review gate

## Observation

On plan `org-empty-review-guard-too-broad` the dispatched phase-3-outline leaf (answer-recording re-entry) reported "phase transitioned: 3-outline done, next 4-plan". `planning-outline.md` assigns that transition to the orchestrator (Step 2c), after the sibling q-gate-validation dispatch and the Step-3 USER REVIEW GATE. The orchestrator had to `set-phase 3-outline` to re-open the phase before re-dispatching the operator's review changes.

## Impact

The status can read `4-plan` while the outline still has pending Q-Gate findings and no operator approval. A resume in that window would route to phase-4-plan and skip the review gate.

## Directive

Remove the `transition --completed 3-outline` call from the phase-3-outline leaf body (or guard it behind an orchestrator-only flag), so the orchestrator is the single writer of that transition, matching the phase-2-refine/phase-4-plan split.
