# `--plan-id` was appended to a script that declares it nowhere

Source: script-failure cluster, notation plan-marshall:manage-run-config:run_config
(exit_code=2, failure_kind=argparse_rejection). 1 occurrence, 6-finalize at 14:55:48.

Rejection: "run_config.py: error: unrecognized arguments: --plan-id
architecture-store-query-truthfulness". The script's top level declares only the verb
set {init, validate, timeout, warning, architecture-refresh, ci-duration,
language-server, derivation-resolver, display-timezone, commit-trailer, cleanup,
cleanup-status} and no --plan-id at all.

## Solution

This is the THIRD variant of the `--plan-id` placement family seen in one run, and the
three prescribe different moves on different surfaces:

- declared at the ROUTER (architecture, ci): the flag goes BEFORE the verb;
- declared on the SUBCOMMAND (most manage-* verbs): it goes AFTER the verb;
- NOT DECLARED AT ALL (run_config): appending it is an outright rejection.

Appending `--plan-id` by rote is therefore wrong one time in three. The surface decides,
never the flag name.

## Impact

One rejection; the underlying confusion produced rejections on three separate scripts
this run.
