# A lane variant that collapses a phase envelope must re-home the folded steps writes

## What happened

On a `planning_lane: light` plan, `phase_handshake capture` FAILED with `pr_title_missing` at **both**
the 2-refine→3-outline boundary and the 3-outline→4-plan boundary. The run had to be unblocked by
authoring `status.metadata.pr_title` by hand.

Root cause: `phase-2-refine` Step 13 is the sole writer of `status.metadata.pr_title`. The light lane
(`phase-3-outline/workflow/light-lane.md`) collapses the refine envelope and folds Step 13 away —
**and supplies no replacement writer.** Every light-lane plan therefore reaches the handshake with the
field unset. Not intermittent: structural, and it fires on every light-lane run.

The same collapse dropped more than one field:

- `references.json` was left without `track` and `affected_files`, and `phase-4-plan` **silently fell
  back** instead of reporting the gap.
- `phases[].2-refine` was left at `in_progress` while the plan reached `6-finalize` — the phase row was
  never closed, so the plan's own `status.json` ended internally inconsistent.

A sibling instance in the same family: `create-pr` Step 3.6 reads a manifest field (`enabled_bots`)
that the composed manifest does not contain — a consumer whose producer does not exist at all.

## Why it matters

The handshake gate was **not** the defect — it refused correctly. The defect is that a lane variant
**silently removed the only producer of a field a downstream consumer requires**.

The failure surfaces two boundaries away from the omission, as an opaque `pr_title_missing`. By then
the producer's identity is no longer visible from the error, so the diagnosis cost is paid every time
rather than once. And where the consumer falls back silently instead of refusing (the
`references.json` case), the omission produces **no error at all** — the run completes on degraded
inputs and reports success.

A collapsed envelope is a legitimate optimization. Dropping a step's *work* while keeping its
*obligations* is what makes it a defect.

## How to apply

- When a lane or mode collapses a phase envelope, **enumerate every `status.metadata` and
  `references.json` field the folded steps WROTE**, and re-home each write into the collapsed lane.
  Either re-home it or explicitly declare the field optional — **doing neither converts a lane choice
  into a guaranteed mid-run failure.**
- Do not rely on a downstream handshake to surface the omission. A gate that fires two boundaries
  later reports a symptom, not a cause.
- **Recurrence signature** — sweep the folded steps for `manage-status update-field` and
  `manage-references set` calls. Any field written there and read later by another phase is a
  producerless-consumer candidate.
- A consumer that **silently falls back** on a missing input is strictly worse than one that refuses:
  the refusal is a bug report, the fallback is a false green. When adding a fallback, log the
  degradation.
- ⛔ Closing the phase row is part of the folded step's obligations too. A collapsed envelope that
  leaves `phases[].{phase}` at `in_progress` produces a status file that contradicts itself, and
  every later reader inherits the contradiction.
