## Context

One 90-minute quota wait; three background sleeps killed by memory pressure; each kill
cost re-noticing + recompute + fresh sleep — recovered by hand each time (correct, but
dependent on remembered state that degrades under exactly the pressure long waits
produce). Recurrence: same shape, 2 kills; `recovery_attempt_cap` 6 binds before the
operator's 10-wait ceiling, with deadline/attempts/ceiling in three separate states.

## Proposed action

Persist the deadline once observed; expose a wait-until verb recomputing the remainder
from wall clock per call (resumable across session restart; carries attempt count so
ceilings stay comparable).
