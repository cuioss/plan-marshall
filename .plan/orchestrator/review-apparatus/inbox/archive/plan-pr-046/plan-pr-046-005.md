# Re-derive the declared footprint when finalize edits widen it

component: plan-marshall:phase-6-finalize
category: improvement
confidence: medium

## Context

The plan declared 23 distinct paths across its five deliverables and shipped 31. Ten of the eleven additions are files no deliverable named at all: `review_completeness.py`, `branch-cleanup.md`, `_build_execute_factory.py`, `api-contract.md`, `generate_executor.py`, `automated-review-lifecycle.md`, and four test files. Every one of them entered during finalize — they are the edits the eight self-review rounds, the simplify step, and the plugin-doctor and deploy-target steps demanded. The outline's declared surface was never re-derived as they landed, so the plan's own record of what it intended to touch ended the run ten files behind what it did touch.

## Root cause

The declared footprint is captured once, at outline, and finalize has no step that reconciles it against the realized footprint as finalize's own edits widen it. Each individual widening was legitimate and reviewed; the gap is that nothing writes the widened surface back.

## Proposed action

Invoke the three-way reconciliation `manage-references` already owns at a finalize step after the last editing step and before the footprint-consuming steps, and either update the declaration or surface the divergence as a reviewed decision. Note the ordering constraint: any reconciliation placed after `branch-cleanup` inherits the merged-base problem, because the realized footprint is no longer recoverable from a base-ref diff at that point.
