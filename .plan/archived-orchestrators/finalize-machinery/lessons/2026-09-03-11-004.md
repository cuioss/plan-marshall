caller invoked `manage-status read --plan-id output-volume-standard --phase 6-finalize`.

## Root cause

`--phase` reads as a natural narrowing flag, and workflow prose talks in phase terms
throughout. But `read` takes no such flag: phase step records already live inside the
`metadata.phase_steps` block the bare `read` returns. Invented-flag half of the "Never
invent script subcommands" recurrence signature — despite a dedicated warning paragraph
in `plan-retrospective/SKILL.md` Step 1.

## Proposed action

1. In `manage-status/SKILL.md`, state on the `read` entry that phase-scoped step records
   are returned inside `metadata.phase_steps` and that `read` accepts no `--phase`.
2. Have the executor guard's rejection message suggest the nearest declared form.
