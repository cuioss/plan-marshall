# Finding: light-lane pre-dispatch 2-refine capture demands a refine artifact

## What happened

Plan `issue-1697` (`planning_lane=light`). After the operator-authorized
bare-transition exemption closed 2-refine and the `2-refine → 3-outline`
metrics boundary was stamped, the paired invariant capture failed:

`phase_handshake capture --plan-id issue-1697 --phase 2-refine`
→ `error: pr_title_missing — metadata.pr_title is absent or empty —
phase-2-refine Step 13 must author and persist a commit-style PR title`.

The light lane never dispatches `phase-2-refine`, so Step 13 never runs
before this capture. Per the branch contract the envelope was not
dispatched. No `--override` was used; the run stopped.

## Why it matters

Same shape as the bare-transition refusal: a pre-dispatch closure step
demands an artifact only the skipped phase body writes. A light plan with no
refine body cannot satisfy the 2-refine capture without either authoring
refine outputs outside the envelope (a phase-boundary violation) or an
explicit workflow-authorized override.

## What is owed (process-compliance)

- Either the light-lane pre-dispatch capture carries a workflow-authorized
  `--override --reason` for the missing refine-authored invariants, or the
  2-refine capture contract exempts the light-lane pre-dispatch closure
  (e.g. capturing only lane-independent rows).
- No workaround applied: no `pr_title` was invented and no `--override`
  was used unilaterally.
