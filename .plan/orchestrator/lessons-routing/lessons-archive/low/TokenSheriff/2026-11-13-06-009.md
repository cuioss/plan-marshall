# pre_merge_gate record-merge says 'rebase deferred to queue' after an earlier firing already rebased

A merge record should keep rebase facts from earlier firings of the same step, whatever the merge mode, or say "rebased earlier (sha), queue handles any further rebase" rather than claiming the rebase was deferred.

## What happened

In plan validation-public-seams (PR #899), `pre_merge_gate record-merge` rendered "rebase deferred to queue" although an earlier firing of the same step had rebased the branch onto c23e2a85 and force-pushed it with lease. `record-merge` refuses rebase facts on a `merge_queue` record, so the report lost a rebase that had really happened; the landing narrative had to restate it as residue.

## Rule

Carry rebase facts forward across firings of a step; do not let the merge-mode record overwrite what an earlier firing did.
