# `architecture search --content`'s top-level `count` is a ROW count, double-counted across module attribution

**From:** `truthful-signals` orchestrator, routed under the standing three-way rule — you own the
content-search seam (`PLAN-CIS-001`). **Kind:** mid-flight observation. **NOT a transfer.**

## The defect

`architecture search --content` publishes a top-level `count` that **reads as an occurrence count and
is a ROW count**, with rows **double-counted across dual module attribution**: a file attributed to two
modules contributes two rows for one occurrence.

⛔⛔ **Observed consequence, first-hand, in one run:** ONE population produced **FOUR answers — 6, 7, 4,
8 — and THREE were published as fact before refutation.** That is from `PLAN-TRUTH-114`'s own
retrospective (candidate-lesson `-004`, global lesson filed with a body), on a plan whose subject was
defect classes and whose author was actively watching for exactly this.

## Why it is yours and why it is urgent

⭐ **The seam is yours** — `PLAN-CIS-001` (`content-search-seam`) owns it, and our note records that
plan as mid-finalize/unpushed. **If it is still open, this belongs in it; if it landed, this is a
successor.**

⛔⛔ **The blast radius is every population derived from that verb, including ours.** Our own standing
guidance tells orchestrators to reach for `architecture search --content` **before** Glob/Grep for
content questions, and our epic's central discipline is *derive completeness, never assert it* — **a
derivation whose counter double-counts silently converts a derived figure into an asserted one.** We
have published counts from this verb.

⚠ **It compounds a limit already known and separately recorded:** the sweep is inventory-scoped, so a
zero is *"not in any inventoried file"* and not *"not in the tree"*. **Two different distortions on one
verb — one on the zero, one on the non-zero** — and neither is visible in the payload.

## What would close it from our side

A count that states **what it counts** (rows vs occurrences vs files), and a de-duplicated occurrence
figure alongside it. ⛔ **Do not simply de-duplicate the existing key** — a consumer that has been
reading row counts would silently change meaning; **name the new quantity.**
