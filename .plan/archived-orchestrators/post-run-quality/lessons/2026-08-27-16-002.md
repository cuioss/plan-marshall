## Context

8 of 33 recorded finalize dispatches terminated in a fatal `error`, consuming
2,189,320 tokens. Per the dispatch-boundary contract, an `error` row is genuinely
non-productive terminal waste — a findings-bearing loop-back is stamped
`returned_with_findings` instead, and this phase recorded 2 of those separately.
So the 2.19M is spend that examined nothing and returned nothing. It is 34% of the
phase's 6,435,116-token dispatch total.

Four of the eight errors cluster inside a 14-second window (17:29:10 to 17:29:23)
immediately followed by a `blocked_session_restart` row, which reads as one
infrastructure event costing four dispatches rather than four independent faults.

## Root cause

Two distinct causes are being folded into one bucket. `retryable_total_tokens` is
recorded as 0 because the `blocked_session_restart` row itself carried no tokens,
while the four errors it plainly caused are attributed to `error_total_tokens`.
The figure that names recoverable waste therefore reads zero on a run whose
largest single waste event was recoverable.

## Proposed action

Attribute an `error` row that falls inside the blast radius of an adjacent
`blocked_session_restart` / `harness_cancellation` to `retryable_total_tokens`,
so the two remedies stay separable. Separately, consider a per-step ceiling on
consecutive error-terminated re-dispatches before the step escalates rather than
re-firing.

⚠ **Do not size that ceiling from this lesson's `failed` counts.** The
`pre-submission-self-review` `failed` outcomes cited here and in the Recurrence
section were findings-bearing rounds mis-recorded as failures; that step now
records `--outcome loop_back --loop-back-target 6-finalize` on its
findings-bearing branch, and a loop-back is a PRODUCTIVE non-completion, not
terminal waste. A ceiling derived from those counts would cap the gate for
working. The ceiling this action asks for is over genuinely
**error-terminated** re-dispatches, which is a strictly smaller population than
the one the old label produced.

## Recurrence — 2026-09-03 (misconfigured-reviewer-name-reads-missing-review)

Same instrument, same phase, and the run confirms the original diagnosis while adding a
second, structural half to it: **the published spend classes do not partition terminal
spend at all.**

6-finalize on PR #1392 recorded 21 dispatch-boundary rows: 17 `step_complete`, 2 `error`,
1 `returned_with_findings`, 1 `blocked_user_review`.

- `error_total_tokens: 456033` — both rows are `pre-submission-self-review`, 11.7 % of the
  phase's 3,753,083 dispatch total. Lower than the source plan's 34 %, but the same class.
- `retryable_total_tokens: 0` — and here correctly zero: no `blocked_session_restart` or
  `harness_cancellation` row exists on this run, so the original "recoverable waste reads
  zero" symptom did not recur.
- **`blocked_user_review`, 187,858 tokens, lands in NEITHER published class.** It is
  `automatic-review` returning `escalate_ask{reason: rate_window_exhausted}` — not waste
  (it correctly refused to mark the step done) but not retryable-infrastructure either. A
  reader summing the two published figures understates terminal-state spend by 187,858 on
  this plan.

So the two classes are not exhaustive over `DISPATCH_TERMINATION_CAUSES`. The original
proposed action re-attributes rows *between* the two buckets; this recurrence says the
bucket set itself is short. Add a third class for operator-blocked terminations, or state
explicitly that the two published figures do not sum to terminal spend and publish the
residual.

The per-step ceiling half of the original proposal also recurs unchanged:
`pre-submission-self-review` fired 5 times with 2 `error` outcomes and no ceiling.

## Recurrence — 2026-09-05 (shipped-guards-assume-the-meta-projects-own-layout)

Third recurrence, the largest by a wide margin — and it **refutes a claim this lesson already
makes about itself.**

6-finalize recorded 24 dispatch-boundary rows: 11 `step_complete`, **11 `error`**,
1 `blocked_user_review`, 1 `returned_with_findings`. `error_total_tokens: 2323435` — 39 % of
the phase's 6,009,594 and 24 % of the whole 9,644,036-token plan. Against the source plan's
34 % and the second recurrence's 11.7 %, this is the highest absolute waste figure recorded
and the highest error-row count (11 vs 8 vs 2).

**All 11 `error` rows are `pre-submission-self-review`** — the step this lesson's ⚠ block
above says "now records `--outcome loop_back --loop-back-target 6-finalize` on its
findings-bearing branch". On this run it did not. The step recorded `outcome=failed` on all
11 findings-bearing rounds (`status.metadata.phase_steps` shows `firing_count: 15` with
`prior_firings` = 11 `failed` + 3 `done`), and the dispatcher stamps `returned_with_findings`
only when `mark-step-done` recorded `loop_back`. So the documented remedy is either not
landed, regressed, or was not reached on this path — and the ⚠ block's warning not to size a
ceiling from `failed` counts is, on this run, protecting a population that the ledger has
once again mislabelled as `error`.

Two consequences follow, and they point in opposite directions:

- The **cap** half of this lesson is now urgent: 11 unbounded re-fires of one step is the
  single largest cost centre of the most expensive plan on record (>= 3.3M tokens including
  the 3 `step_complete` firings), and the round loop closed OUT OF BUDGET and NOT converged
  at 12 rounds rather than because a ceiling stopped it.
- The **attribution** half is now wrong in the other direction from the source plan. Here
  `error_total_tokens` **over**-states waste: those 11 dispatches filed 26 real findings that
  were all fixed. They bought a great deal of detection. Filed separately as lesson
  `2026-09-05-07-002`.
