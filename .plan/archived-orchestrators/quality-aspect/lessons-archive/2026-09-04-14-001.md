# Compare [DISPATCH] lines per firing, not per step, in check-dispatch-audit

## Context

On plan `domain-glob-seeding`, `check-dispatch-audit` reported `missing_dispatch_emission: 0` over an `evaluated_population` of 16 and `findings[0]` — a clean audit. An independent read of the same `work.log` shows the `[DISPATCH]` channel disagreeing with the envelope-completion channel in both directions:

| step | `[DISPATCH]` lines | `execution-context.{step}) Complete` lines | `firing_count` |
|------|-------------------:|------------------------------------------:|---------------:|
| `default:pre-submission-self-review` | 1 | 2 | 2 |
| `plan-marshall:automatic-review` | 1 | 2 | 2 |
| `default:finalize-step-simplify` | 2 | 1 | 2 |

Two steps re-fired after a `failed` / `loop_back` outcome and dispatched a fresh envelope with no second `[DISPATCH]` line; a third emitted two lines against one envelope completion.

## Root cause

`dispatch_coverage` classifies a population of **steps**, not **firings**. It reads `status.metadata.phase_steps["6-finalize"]` and buckets each step name once into `dispatched` / `ran_inline` / `no_evidence`. A step that fired twice and emitted one `[DISPATCH]` line satisfies "has at least one line", so it is bucketed `dispatched` and contributes no shortfall. The per-step framing makes an under-emitting re-fire structurally undetectable.

The sibling `shape_violation` check cannot cover the gap either: it pairs the decision-log `effort resolve-target` records against the `[DISPATCH]` work-log lines, and both are written by the same seam — the corroboration limit its own standards doc warns about. When the seam does not fire, neither surface records anything, and the check reports `0/19` clean.

`channel_completeness` did notice something was wrong (`ratio: 0.421`, `confidence: low`) but grades confidence rather than emitting a finding, so the audit still returned `findings[0]`.

## Proposed action

Add a per-firing arm to `dispatch_coverage` in `marketplace/bundles/plan-marshall/skills/plan-retrospective/scripts/check-dispatch-audit.py`:

- For every step bucketed `dispatched`, read `firing_count` from its `status.metadata.phase_steps` entry (absent reads as `1`, matching the omit-when-absent convention `mark-step-done` documents).
- Count the `[DISPATCH]` lines whose `workflow=` names that step's implementor.
- Report the signed delta per step, and emit a `missing_dispatch_emission` finding when the delta is negative and an `excess_dispatch_emission` finding when positive.

Both inputs are already in hand — the script reads `status.json` and `logs/` today, and `firing_count` / `prior_firings` are written at the `mark-step-done` site. Publish the per-firing population alongside the per-step one rather than replacing it, so the existing `evaluated_population: 16` keeps its meaning.

The paired producer-side fix belongs in `phase-6-finalize`: the re-fire path taken from the resumable re-entry check must emit its own `[DISPATCH]` line, exactly as the first firing does.
