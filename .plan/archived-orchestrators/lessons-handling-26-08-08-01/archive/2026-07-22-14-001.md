# q-gate deliverable-hash segmentation leaves the last deliverable's end boundary undefined

## Problem

`plan-marshall:plan-marshall/workflow/q-gate-validation.md` Step 3.5 defines a deliverable block for hashing as running "through the line before the next deliverable heading", but leaves the **last** deliverable's end boundary undefined.

The observed convention (reverse-engineered from a stored `work/deliverable-hashes.toon` by byte-exact reproduction) extends the final deliverable to EOF. That means the last deliverable's hash also fingerprints the trailing `## Approach` and `## Risks and Mitigations` sections.

## Consequence

An edit confined to `## Approach` or `## Risks and Mitigations` spuriously invalidates the last deliverable's content-hash skip gate, forcing an unnecessary re-validation of a deliverable whose own text never changed. The whole-outline `__whole_outline__` row already covers document-level change detection, so the per-deliverable row buys nothing extra by extending to EOF.

## Directive

Define the last deliverable's end boundary explicitly in q-gate-validation.md Step 3.5: terminate the final deliverable block at the next top-level `## ` heading (i.e. the first section following the deliverables), not at EOF. Any change to the hashing convention must be paired with a one-time re-emission of stored `work/deliverable-hashes.toon` rows, since a convention change otherwise invalidates every stored hash at once.

Observed during the outline phase of plan `arm-pytest-gates-remeasure-yardsticks`.
