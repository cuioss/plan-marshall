# Barrier-ask-override precedent for spend-capped stale review bot

component: plan-marshall:phase-6-finalize

status: active

## Pattern

When a required bot is provably incapacitated (stale participation + failure
notice) and every other signal is green, escalate to the operator and persist
the override as a merge-authorization record bound to HEAD and gap class —
never as an undocumented skip. Future plans hitting a silent required
reviewer have a recorded precedent: escalate, bind the authorization, land,
and keep the gap auditable.

## Provenance

Promoted from process-compliance inbox `phase-gates-009.md`: automatic-review
force-doned with cuioss-review-bot stale (backend spend-capped) while
CodeRabbit fully reviewed and CI was green; operator merge-anyway recorded as
HEAD-bound `barrier-ask-override` (plan phase-gates, PR #1540 as 433d0a6).
