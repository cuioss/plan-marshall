# Announcing continuation and then ending the turn is a halt, not progress

## Context

Plan `domain-glob-seeding` ran with both unattended-execution knobs set:

- `final_merge_without_asking: true` (`default:branch-cleanup` step-params)
- loop-back continuation enabled (`loop_back_iteration: 1` was consumed and re-entered without an operator gate)

Twice during `6-finalize`, the orchestrator wrote that it would proceed to the next step and then **ended the turn** with no question asked, no gate raised, and no blocking condition present. Both times the operator had to type "why did you stop?" to resume a run that was configured to need no operator at all.

The stall is visible in the wall-clock record as a gap with no logged call between two adjacent steps — the widest being `13:45:20` (`branch-cleanup` complete) to `14:02:28` (`finalize-step-deploy-target` executing): 17 minutes of nothing, between two steps with no dependency, no gate, and no wait.

The cost is not the wall time alone. This plan's metrics read **12h54m idle against 2h8m worked**; a phase that stops without saying it is waiting turns unattended execution into supervised execution and silently inverts what the knobs were set for.

## Root cause

Narrating an intention is not performing it. The finalize FOR loop's continuation is a *tool call*, and a turn that ends after the narration has performed no continuation — but the narration reads, to the model that wrote it, as though the step were underway. There is no structural signal distinguishing "I said I would continue and did" from "I said I would continue and stopped", because both produce identical prose and the second produces no log line at all.

The `without_asking` knobs remove the *question* but nothing enforces the *action*: no check asserts that a turn ending inside `6-finalize` either completed a step, recorded a blocked outcome, or raised an operator gate. A silent stop satisfies none of the three and is caught by nothing.

## Proposed action

1. **State the rule in `phase-6-finalize`**: a turn may end inside the finalize loop only when one of three things is true — the phase reached its terminal step, a step recorded `--outcome blocked_user_review` / `blocked_session_restart`, or an `AskUserQuestion` is actually pending. Announcing the next step is not one of them. When the next step is known and no gate is pending, **issue its dispatch in the same turn**.
2. **Make the stall observable.** The finalize dispatcher already emits `[STEP] … Executing step:` on entry and `[STEP] … Completed step:` on exit. A turn that ends between the two, with no `blocked_*` outcome recorded, is a detectable stall — surface it as a `logging_gap_analysis` category so a retrospective finds it without an operator report.
3. **Cross-reference the existing halt-reporting lesson** `2026-09-03-11-006` ("Say what the run is waiting for when a finalize gate halts the session"): that lesson governs a *legitimate* halt reporting its reason. This one governs an *illegitimate* halt with no reason to report. They are complementary, not duplicates — a stop that states no reason because it has none is the case neither currently covers.
