# A finalize step that runs before the merge window cannot capture what the merge window reveals

## Status — the ordering half is CLOSED; the disclosure rule is not

`lessons-capture` used to run before `branch-cleanup`, the step where the merge happens and where a
whole class of defects becomes observable: merge-gate misbehaviour, participation-barrier verdicts,
worktree teardown failures, post-merge review arrivals. On one run that stranded five separately
observed defects, which reached the epic only because the operator relayed them by hand.

**That mis-ordering can no longer occur.** The post-run-review role is now a declared frontmatter
fact on each finalize-step doc, governed by two predicates (backward-looking output ∧ post-merge
evidence dependency), and its membership set is derived from that fact through the same discovery
call the registry uses rather than hand-listed. A derivation guard asserts, over the derived
population and against a dynamically resolved merge-gate order, that no member is ordered at or
before the gate — so the recurrence signature this lesson asked for ("can an event this step reports
on occur after it runs?") is enforced over the whole roster rather than remembered. The three
mis-ordered steps were moved behind the gate accordingly.

**Do not re-stage the reorder, the roster derivation, or the recurrence-signature sweep — all three
are done.** What remains below is the one un-enacted residual.

## Residual — a completeness claim still need not name its observation window

A reporting step placed before the events it reports on does not report *less* — it reports
**confidently and incompletely**. Its output reads as the run's complete residue. Nothing in the
artifact says "observations after step N are not represented here", so a reader treats an empty tail
as "nothing further happened."

Ordering closes that for the post-run-review set. It closes nothing for any *other* step whose
output is a completeness claim over a window narrower than the run: no contract requires such a step
to state its window, and no guard checks that it does. The claim stays unscoped and therefore
unfalsifiable.

## How to apply

- When a step's output is a *completeness* claim ("here is this run's residue"), **state its
  observation window in the artifact**. A scoped claim that names its scope is honest; the same claim
  unscoped is a false completeness signal.
- ⛔ **Do not fix this by asking the operator to relay.** A hand-relay works exactly as long as the
  operator happens to notice, which is not a mechanism.
