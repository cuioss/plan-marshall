# Replace the standalone sleep in the review completion poll with a blocking wait

## Context

On the second `automatic-review` pass of plan `plan-pr-078-review-bot-fleet-opt-in` (PR #1704, HEAD f80ed122b), the workflow's completion-aware poll needed to pace itself between `bot_completion` reads. The documented pacing call is a standalone `sleep 30`. The harness refused it. The leaf did not route around the refusal; it polled CodeRabbit several times (four by the leaf's report; the WARNING line was written after two), spaced only by other documented steps, saw `in_progress: true` each time, logged a WARNING, and marked the step `done`. CodeRabbit's incremental review of the new commit range had not published. The participation predicate still passed because CodeRabbit's earlier inline comments count as participation. The pre-merge comment barrier later read clean and the PR merged.

## Root cause

The workflow depends on a foreground `sleep` that a dispatched leaf is not permitted to run, so `review_completion_poll_timeout_seconds` (600 s) is unreachable there: the poll ends when the leaf runs out of other steps, not when the bound elapses. And the participation check answers "did this bot ever comment on this PR", not "did this bot finish reviewing this HEAD", so an in-progress re-review satisfies it.

## Proposed action

- Add a bounded blocking wait to the CI abstraction (a `bot_completion` variant that waits server-side-polled up to a timeout, in the shape `pr wait-for-comments` already has) and replace the standalone `sleep` with it.
- Make the step's outcome say what happened: when a required bot's check-run is still in progress at the end of the poll, record that in `display_detail` and in a typed fact rather than a WARNING line only.
- Have the pre-merge barrier re-read `bot_completion` for required bots and refuse a merge while one is `in_progress` for the merge-candidate SHA.

## Evidence

- aspect: permission_prompt_analysis - Bash `sleep 30` refused by the harness (severity warning, confidence high)
- aspect: log_analysis - work log WARNING 679664: "Completion-aware poll: bot coderabbit still IN_PROGRESS after 2 polls; the standalone sleep pacing call was refused by the harness, so the poll could not be paced to the review_completion_poll_timeout_seconds=600s bound"
