# A review-bot fix to a security mechanism is not evidence the property got stronger: audit the fix itself at the mechanism's edges

Source: PLAN-V02-06 (#528), candidate lesson 006.

## Observation

During finalize a review-bot finding about a security hardening mechanism was accepted and the fix was committed in the PR. The next code-review round found that the fix had partly weakened the property the finding was meant to protect, and the operator kept the change and documented its behaviour. "Implements the suggestion" had been read as "the property is stronger".

## Rule

- Before committing a bot-suggested fix to a security mechanism, have the security audit review the fix itself with the lens of the original finding: does it add a new observable difference or a new impact, and does it hold at the edges of the mechanism (small values, boundaries, rounding)?
- Record the result of that audit in the finding's resolution.
- A step-wise or quantised timing or size rule moves the signal; check whether it separates small deviations more than before.

## Apply

Any security-hardening plan that triages bot findings against a timing, size, count or error-shape mechanism (V02-50 and V02-07 first).
