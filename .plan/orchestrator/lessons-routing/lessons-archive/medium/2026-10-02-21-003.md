# Block the outline-to-plan transition while Q-Gate findings are pending

## Context

In plan `unified-sync-all-harnesses` the outline Q-Gate failed deliverable 4 (architecture mismatch in its Design notes) and left 3 findings pending. The plan then went through two phase transitions with those findings still pending:

- 3-outline to 4-plan at 2026-10-02T11:59:00Z, after the operator answered the outline review with "Proceed to create tasks".
- 4-plan to 5-execute at 12:25:00Z, automatically (`execute_without_asking=true`).

The handshake recorded `pending_findings_blocking_count: 3` at both boundaries. The findings were resolved at 13:50Z, during execute, and only because the operator noticed the session was running under Haiku and asked for the structure to be revisited. The outline and the task plan themselves were produced by pinned higher-level envelopes and were confirmed unchanged; the part that ran under the weaker model was the orchestration around them.

## Root cause

OBSERVED: the count named "blocking" is captured at each boundary and compared as drift afterwards; it did not stop either transition. Acting on a failed Q-Gate deliverable before the review gate is left to the orchestrator's judgement, and nothing mechanical backs that judgement up.

Not established: whether the transition has a pending-findings check that was bypassed, or has none at these two boundaries. The execute-to-finalize transition does have one (the execute leaves reported "the pending gate permits (0 pending)").

A second gap made the cause invisible: nothing records which model the orchestrator itself runs. The switch from Haiku to Opus is known only from one decision line the orchestrator wrote after the operator pointed it out.

## Proposed action

1. Make `transition --completed 3-outline` and `--completed 4-plan` refuse while phase-scoped Q-Gate findings are pending, with the same check the execute exit already applies, and require a recorded override to pass.
2. Show the pending Q-Gate count in the outline review prompt, so "Proceed to create tasks" is an informed answer.
3. Record the orchestrator's model at init and on resume (work log plus status metadata), and warn when it is below the level the phase dispatches are pinned to.
