## What happened

`manage-status archive` ran before `mark-step-done` (inverse of mandated order);
archive moved status.json; mark-step-done failed file_not_found; 22/23 steps recorded,
23rd unaddable forever. Structurally unique: the only step destroying the store its own
record writes to, at order 1100 with no next boundary — silent by construction (archive
doesn't check the record; failed mark-step-done exits 0 via convention; artefact records
absence). Same inverted run skipped the foreign-PR gate (no-op here; would have archived
past pushed_no_pr elsewhere). Document not at fault — constraint prose-enforced only.

## Proposed remedy

Self-enforcing `archive` (refuse with named code when the terminal step row absent), or
atomic `--archive-step` (record + move in one op). Retrospective scan available: archived
plans whose phase_steps lack the execution.toon terminal step were archived out of order.
