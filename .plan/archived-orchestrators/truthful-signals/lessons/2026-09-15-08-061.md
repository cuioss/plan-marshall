# The CI router rejected --plan-id written after the verb, and printed the caller's own fixed command

Source: script-failure cluster, notation plan-marshall:tools-integration-ci:ci
(exit_code=2, failure_kind=argparse_rejection). 1 occurrence, 6-finalize at 16:46:45.

Rejection: "ci.py: error: unrecognized arguments: --plan-id
architecture-store-query-truthfulness / note: --plan-id is a top-level flag and belongs
BEFORE the subcommand (verb), not after it. The flag exists — it is only in the wrong
position. Move it ahead of the verb, e.g.
`... plan-marshall:tools-integration-ci:ci --plan-id <id> pr view`."

## Solution

The error message here is already close to ideal — it names the flag, states the rule,
and prints the caller's own invocation with the flag moved. The call still failed once,
which bounds how much a better message can achieve on its own.

The remaining ambiguity is real and not the caller's invention: on `ci`, the router
consumes --plan-id only ahead of the FIRST verb token, and ci's body-consumer verbs
(prepare-body / prepare-comment) declare their OWN required --plan-id AFTER the verb. So
the same script wants the flag in both positions depending on the verb, and a caller who
learns "before the verb on ci" is then wrong on prepare-body.

## Impact

One rejection on a surface where the correct position is genuinely verb-dependent.
