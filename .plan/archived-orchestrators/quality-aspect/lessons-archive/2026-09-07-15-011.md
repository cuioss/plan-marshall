# The lessons-capture Signal Gate buckets script failures by the emitting notation, not the failing one

# Observation to verify: the lessons-capture Signal Gate forwarded 1 script-failure cluster where the phase log holds 3 distinct failing notations

**Status**: VERIFIED AND UPGRADED TO A DEFECT by the test-quality orchestrator, 2026-09-07. The original text is preserved below; the verification is appended at the end. The undercount is larger than observed and the root cause is identified. Recorded for the orchestrator to confirm or refute — the leaf that observed it cannot check the gate's derivation without recomputing the signal it is contractually barred from recomputing.

## What was observed

The `lessons-capture` dispatch forwarded `signal_script_failure_clusters_count: 1`.

Reading the `6-finalize` work log for the records behind that signal (reading the records is permitted; re-deriving the count is not) surfaced **three** distinct failing notations, all `failure_kind=argparse_rejection`, all timestamped **before** the gate was evaluated:

- `plan-marshall:workflow-integration-github:github_pr` — `2026-09-07T06:13:08Z`
- `plan-marshall:manage-references:manage-references` — `2026-09-07T15:08:36Z`
- `plan-marshall:manage-solution-outline:manage-solution-outline` — `2026-09-07T15:11:09Z`

The `lessons-capture` step began at `2026-09-07T15:18:43Z` and dispatched at `15:19:19Z`, so all three records existed at gate-evaluation time. The gate's definition is "number of **distinct failing script notations**" across `[FAILED]`, `[ERROR] ... script_failure`, and `voluntary_checkpoint → error` markers, with union dedup **by distinct notation** — three distinct notations should dedup to three.

## Why it may still be correct

Explanations the leaf could not rule out, listed so the check is cheap:

1. The gate may scope its log scan to a phase other than, or narrower than, the whole run (e.g. `5-execute` only) — in which case 1 is right for its scope and the count's *name* is what misleads.
2. The gate may dedup by something other than notation (failure kind, or step) despite the documented rule.
3. The gate may read the `script` log rather than the `work` log, and the two may not carry the same markers.

## Why it is worth checking anyway

The gate is a **skip decision**. At all-zero signals this workflow body is never dispatched at all. A cluster counter that under-reports by 3x is therefore not merely a cosmetic number — it is the thing that decides whether the run's script failures are ever looked at. An undercount that reaches zero silently discards the whole class.

This is the same shape as the sweep's own rule-undercount finding already routed to this epic (a rule reporting only the first match per segment cannot serve as its own worklist), at a different site: **a derived count consumed as a gate must be checked against an independent enumeration at least once.** If the gate is confirmed correct here, that check is what makes its zeros trustworthy.

## Orchestrator verification (test-quality analyze, 2026-09-07) — CONFIRMED, and worse

**The undercount is 6x, not 3x.** The leaf read only the `6-finalize` section and saw
three notations. The archived run's whole `logs/work.log` carries **14**
`[ERROR] ... script_failure` lines over **7 distinct failing notations**:
`manage-references`, `manage-findings`, `manage-solution-outline`, `manage-files`,
`architecture`, `github_pr`, `manage-status`. The last fired at 15:26:24Z, after the
gate evaluated at 15:18:43Z, so **6 distinct notations existed at gate-evaluation
time**. The gate forwarded **1**.

**Both of the leaf's own alternative explanations are refuted.** Hypothesis 1
(narrower phase scope) would yield 3, not 1 — `github_pr` 06:13:08Z,
`manage-references` 15:08:36Z and `manage-solution-outline` 15:11:09Z are all inside
`6-finalize` and all precede the gate. Hypothesis 3 (wrong log) is refuted because the
records are in the `work` log, which is exactly the log
`manage-logging read --type work` returns.

**Root cause, located in the instruction text.** `phase-6-finalize/SKILL.md` § Signal 3
says to bucket each marker line "by the distinct failing script notation (**the
`bundle:skill:script` token in the line**)". Every `script_failure` line carries TWO
tokens of that shape:

    [ERROR] (plan-marshall:execute-script:2) script_failure notation=plan-marshall:manage-solution-outline:manage-solution-outline exit_code=2 ...

The first is the EMITTING context and is identical on every line; the second, behind
`notation=`, is the FAILING script. "The token in the line" is ambiguous exactly where
it must not be, and the reading that takes the first token collapses all 14 lines to
one notation — reproducing the observed `1` exactly.

**Fix**: name the field. Bucket by the `notation=` value, never by "the
`bundle:skill:script` token in the line". One clause, at the authoring site.

**Why this matters more than a wrong number**: the gate is a SKIP decision. At all-zero
signals `lessons-capture` is never dispatched. A counter that collapses every distinct
failure to one is one step away from collapsing to zero, and an undercount that reaches
zero silently discards the entire script-failure lesson class.
