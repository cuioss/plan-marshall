# CodeRabbit's one-review-per-hour quota: batch fixes, group pushes before the bot round, and count the shared quota when pairing plans

## Problem

CodeRabbit, a required review bot, includes one review per hour (its review bodies say "up to 1 included review per hour; 0 remain after this review"). In this repo's quality-report epic that quota stalled every plan: PLAN-01 (PR #830), PLAN-02 (#832), PLAN-03 (#833), PLAN-04 (#834), PLAN-06 (#840) and PLAN-07 (#844) all hit it. A review-driven fix commit or a late docs commit moves HEAD, the bot is `refused_awaitable (quota)` for the new head, and the pre-merge barrier reports `participation_complete=false`. Plans then wait about an hour per round, or merge under a `barrier-ask-override`. Two plans running at once share the single account quota: PLAN-06 waited roughly two hours behind PLAN-07's rate window. After a content-identical rebase the incremental reviewer answered "No files to review", which is not a clean review.

## Solution

Treat the quota as a scarce resource. Batch every actionable comment of a review round (inline and nitpick) into one fix push so a round spends one slot. Group all finalize pushes (formatting rewrites, ADR drafts) before the bot round so its one review lands on the final head. Decide early, not at the merge barrier, whether the override will be needed. After a content-identical rebase, treat "No files to review" as "nothing to diff", not as reviewed, and use the registry `--escalated` full-review command. When pairing plans, count the shared quota as a cost.

## Impact

Hours of waiting per plan, repeated operator escalation, and merges that proceed without a bot review of the final head.
