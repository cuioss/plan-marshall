# ci-verify.md prescribes verification-feedback hand-offs for producers that verification-feedback.md rejects


Source: observation from the orchestrating session of plan `jwks-unknown-kid-refresh` (PR #430).

Two documents in phase-6-finalize contradict each other.

- `standards/ci-verify.md` prescribes one `verification-feedback` hand-off per producer for CI findings, naming the producers `ci-verify-policy` and `ci-verify-cancelled`.
- `verification-feedback.md` does not accept those producers and answers `unknown_producer`.

Following the first document therefore always ends in a refusal by the second.

## What happened in this run

Two hand-offs were made as prescribed and both were rejected. They cost about 570K tokens and produced nothing. The orchestrator then resolved the three CI findings itself.

## Candidate lesson

The set of producers `verification-feedback.md` accepts and the set `ci-verify.md` tells the caller to send must be the same set. Either register `ci-verify-policy` and `ci-verify-cancelled` as accepted producers, or change `ci-verify.md` to name the route that actually handles those findings. A test asserting that every producer named in `ci-verify.md` is in the accepted set would catch the drift.

Until it is fixed: do not hand CI-verify findings from these two producers to `verification-feedback`; resolve them in the main context.

## Impact

About 570K tokens wasted per run that reaches this path, plus the delay of two full hand-offs. Recurs for every plan with a CI policy or cancelled-run finding.
