## G3 — Extend the rule to the narration forms D1 was told to sweep

- **Kind:** omission
- **Severity:** medium
- **Where:** `marketplace/bundles/pm-plugin-development/skills/plugin-doctor/scripts/_analyze_incident_reference_in_docs.py:124-137` — `_TERM_OF_ART_RE` and `_PATTERNS`
- **What is wrong:** `_TERM_OF_ART_RE` requires the incident noun to follow the reference (`#\d{3,4}\s+(?:[A-Za-z]+-[A-Za-z]+\s+)?(?:failure mode|signature|…)`), and no pattern covers dated or version-pinned narration. Re-executed during adversarial review against a thirteen-form fixture, the rule fires on the two ref-then-noun forms and misses every other, including three the plan named: `the failure mode #990 closed cannot recur` (noun **before** ref — 0 findings *with or without* backticks, so this form is missed for a reason independent of G2), `as of 2026-07 the check is green`, and `before 0.1.1240 the writer emitted both keys`. D1's brief was explicit: *"Widen well beyond the seed pattern to cover bare `#NNNN` in prose, pull-request URLs, dated phrasings ('as of 2026-07'), and version-pinned narration ('before 0.1.1240')."* D1's sweep looked for those forms; D4's rule cannot see them. **The dated form has a live witness:** `marketplace/bundles/plan-marshall/skills/tools-permission-doctor/standards/permission-architecture.md:55` reads `As of 2025-10-27:` and opens a four-bullet snapshot of the permission model inside a normative standards document (see G6 — it was already there at `6792510a~1` and D1 reported this form as zero-in-tree).
