# Keep review-bot commit statuses out of the CI-complete check set

The CI arm of `ci_complete_precondition` timed out with only the `CodeRabbit` commit status incomplete. That status is posted by a review bot to show review progress — it is not a CI workflow check, and the real CI workflows had already finished. The pipeline already tracks review-bot completion separately (automatic-review, bot_completion), and the per-signal ratchet design (SKILL.md:230) keeps CI/review/Sonar waits on independent arms — so a review-bot status in the CI set contradicts the documented separation.

## Solution

Leave review-bot commit statuses (CodeRabbit and any other configured review bot) out of the `ci_complete_precondition` check set, matching the status context against the configured review-bot registry. A slow or rate-limited bot then cannot time out the CI precondition, and the failure is reported where it belongs instead of sending the operator to look at CI.

## Impact

One timed-out precondition per occurrence, misreported as a CI problem.
