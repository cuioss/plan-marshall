# A commit made outside a per-deliverable settlement needs its change-ledger row when it is made, or the 5-execute exit gate refuses

Every commit made on a plan's worktree branch outside a per-deliverable settlement, for example one made while an orchestrator verification handback is being handled, needs its own change-ledger row at the time it is made; otherwise the 5-execute exit gate refuses the transition later with `execute_commits_unrecorded`.

## What happened

In plan jwks-loader-gateway-needs, `manage-status transition --completed 5-execute` was refused twice (2026-11-13T14:20:31Z and 14:20:37Z) with `error=execute_commits_unrecorded signature=bookkeeping_gap`: one of nine worktree-branch commits (`0d6b93bb921c7668dceae59895cdc47b3a54e19e`) had no `kind=change` ledger row (recorded_count=8, branch_commit_count=9). The commit was made between the TASK-11 per-deliverable commit (2d71ac40) and the re-entry, apparently while the orchestrator-tier verification handback was being handled, and no writer settled its row. The phase did not advance until the row was settled.

## Rule

- A commit outside a Step 10a per-deliverable settlement gets its change-ledger row when it is made, by whoever makes it.
- The verification-handback commit path must name the writer that records that row, so the 5-execute exit gate does not find the gap.
