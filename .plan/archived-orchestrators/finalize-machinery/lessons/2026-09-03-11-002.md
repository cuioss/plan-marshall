## Observed

In the phase-6 settle band, `default:pre-submission-self-review` declares `order: 7`
and `default:finalize-step-simplify` declares `order: 9`. Both are `head_dependent:
true`. The dispatcher iterates the manifest in ascending order, so on any run where
simplify edits source, self-review examines H1, simplify commits to H2, and nothing
re-runs self-review against H2. The dispatcher's HEAD-dependent re-fire table only
consults `head_at_completion` on re-entry; a forward pass never revisits an earlier
index. Plan `always-on-is-not-a-resolve` demonstrates it: simplify made a
production-Python edit after self-review recorded clean.

## Why it matters

`finalize-step-simplify` exists to REWRITE code late, scheduled after the step that
structurally reviews rewrites. Self-review has no downstream backstop (unlike
`push`'s freshness precondition), so its stale verdict reaches the merge with no signal.

## Directive

Close the ordering hole: re-order (simplify ahead of self-review), re-fire on advance
(after item-5f commits, re-fire completed head_dependent steps via verdict_currency),
or minimum (freshness backstop reporting stale verdicts). Note option 1 alone is
insufficient per the #1426 recurrence (loop-back re-push staled the gate with simplify
playing no part); only re-fire on advance keys off HEAD rather than ordering. Cause 2
(multi-round reviewed tree) needs per-round tree identity or a distinct `multi_tree`
exclusion reason.
