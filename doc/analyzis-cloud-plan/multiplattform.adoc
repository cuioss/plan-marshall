= Cloud-run quality analysis: `multiplattform`
:toc: left
:toclevels: 3
:sectnums:

Analysis of the `multiplattform` plans executed as standalone cloud runs.

[IMPORTANT]
====
**Measured at repository HEAD `ec26306e`, 2026-08-24**, on branch `chore/ingest-multiplattform-epic`.
The ground-truth verification the deliverable figures rest on was taken one commit earlier, at
`2cd1a19c` — the state of `main` when the epic was ingested. The corpus is at
`.plan/local/orchestrator/multiplattform/archive/`.

This document carries a measurement instant, which the project documentation standard otherwise
forbids. The deviation is deliberate and is the subject of the analysis.
====

[WARNING]
====
**This report is the weakest of the four, and the reasons are structural. Read them before reading a
single figure.**

. **n = 3 plans, 13 deliverables.** `truthful-signals` had 45, `code-intelligence-substrate` 37,
  `review-apparatus` 13. Every ratio here is over a denominator small enough that a single plan moves
  it by a third. No percentage in this report should be treated as an estimate of a rate.
. **No plan in this corpus has a `verification.md` or a `gaps.md`.** The README's preconditions
  require both. ⇒ **The gap count — the headline figure of all three sibling reports — does not exist
  for this epic and was not reconstructed.**
. **The cross-lane comparison is out of scope by operator instruction**: this epic has no plans that
  ran through the local plan-marshall lifecycle. Steps 4 and 6 of the method are therefore not
  performed. Two local plans appear in <<_validation>> for **classifier validation only** — that is a
  calibration of the instrument, not a comparison of the lanes.

⇒ **The three-way adjudication rests on a narrower evidence base than in the sibling reports**, and
§ 8 states which candidate explanations this corpus can and cannot speak to.
====

== The instrument gap, stated before any number

This is the single most important methodological fact about this report.

The sibling reports measure deliverable fidelity from a `verification.md` produced by a **separate,
operator-issued verification call** — a third billed pass that sweeps each deliverable against a
checklist and files `gaps.md`. **That pass was never run for any `multiplattform` plan.**

What exists instead is the **ingestion ground-truth pass**: five read-only sub-agents that re-derived
every deliverable from the implementing source at `2cd1a19c`, producing
`.plan/local/orchestrator/multiplattform/landings/PLAN-0{1,2,3}.md`.

[cols="2,3,3",options="header"]
|===
| | Sibling reports' instrument | This report's instrument

| Artifact | `verification.md` + `gaps.md` | `landings/PLAN-NN.md`
| Produced by | separate operator-issued verification call | the orchestrator's ingestion pass
| Question asked | "does the tree satisfy this deliverable, on four axes / by prose verdict — and what else does a sweep find?" | "did each deliverable land as specified, re-derived from the implementing source?"
| Scope beyond the plan's claims | **yes** — adjacent surfaces, checklist sweep | **no** — claim-relative only
| Yields a gap count | yes | **no**
|===

[CAUTION]
====
**Which zero the gap count is.** *Could not look.* No verification pass with a gap-filing brief was
ever run against these three plans, so there is no population to count. This is **not** "swept and
found nothing", and it must never be read as the epic scoring zero gaps.

⛔ **Consequently this report publishes no gaps-per-deliverable figure and its deliverable-fidelity
figure must not be placed beside any sibling report's.** The claim-relative measurements in § 4 and
§ 5 are sound within this corpus and travel nowhere else.
====

⚠ **A second divergence from the method, recorded rather than silently accepted.** The README's
precondition names `cloud-runs/{NNN}-{slug}/` as the ingested corpus path. This epic's ingestion used
`archive/{NNN}-{slug}/` and split the unrun specs into `archive/original-staged-specs/`. The layout
differs because this ingestion also had five *unrun* plans to place, which the sibling ingestions did
not. No figure depends on the path; it is stated so a reproducing script aims correctly.

== Population, and the exclusion that removed nothing

Cloud PRs come in two kinds and only one belongs in a quality analysis: **plan work** (a run of an
authored `plan.md` under the `cloud-plan-lane` rule-set) is analysed; **fixes to the
`cloud-plan-lane` skill itself** are excluded, because they were never governed by the contract this
report evaluates.

Criterion: a PR is a lane-fix when **the only production file it touches is
`.claude/skills/cloud-plan-lane/SKILL.md`** (plan documents under `doc/plans/` are not production).
Each PR number was matched **anchored to the end of the subject line**, per the method's warning.

| Signal | n

| Commits touching `cloud-plan-lane/SKILL.md` | 46
| Of those, lane-fixes (that file is the only production file) | 40
| Lane-fixes **inside this corpus** | **0**
| Corpus PRs touching that file at all, for any reason | **0**
|===

== Deliverable fidelity — claim-relative, and clean

Measured from the three landing records. A deliverable is `as-specified` when the ingestion pass
re-derived it from the implementing source and found it present and behaving as claimed;
`shipped-modified` when it landed with a disposition the plan did not specify.

[cols="1,1,1,1,1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Plan | PR | Deliv | as-specified | modified | Verdict

| `010` | #1291 | 4 | 4 | 0 | fully landed
| `020` | #1313 | 4 | 4 | 0 | fully landed
| `030` | #1319 | 5 | 4 | 1 | fully landed; D5 partly a no-op, disclosed by the run itself
| *TOTAL* | *3* | *13* | *12* | *1* | *3 of 3 fully landed*
|===

* **12 of 13 deliverables (92.3%) landed as specified. 0 fidelity failures.**
* **0 of 3 plans had a deliverable the tree contradicted.**
* **No deliverable was silently descoped** between `plan.md` and `report-01.md` in any of the three —
  checked explicitly, in both directions, per plan.

[IMPORTANT]
====
⛔ **This 92.3% must not be read against `review-apparatus`'s 16.1% clean-verdict rate or
`truthful-signals`' 20.4% fidelity-failure rate.** Three instruments, three questions:

* `truthful-signals` scored four boolean axes **plus an adjacent-surface sweep**.
* `review-apparatus` recorded a **prose verdict** in which "met, with a named shortfall" counts as a
  shortfall — 29 of its 56 deliverables sit in that cell.
* This report asked only **"did it land as specified?"** — the *narrowest* of the three. A
  `multiplattform` deliverable that landed with a named residual is counted `as-specified` here and
  would very plausibly have scored a shortfall in `review-apparatus`.

⇒ **The high rate here is substantially an artefact of the narrowest brief, not evidence of the best
work.** Section 5 is where this corpus's residue actually surfaces.
====

== Where the residue is: 17 items the runs did not close

The ingestion pass files residual work in each landing record's `## Follow-Ups`. This is the nearest
analogue to a `gaps.md` that this corpus has — **and it is not the same measurement**: it is
claim-relative and un-severity-graded, where a `gaps.md` is checklist-driven and severity-graded.

[cols="2,1,4",options="header"]
|===
| Plan | Residual items | Character of the residue

| `010` | 6 | four ABC operations with no decline path; OpenCode `metrics_capture` reporting success while persisting nothing; a target enumeration relocated rather than removed; registration lockstep-*checked* rather than single-sourced
| `020` | 5 | `marketplace/targets/` outside the lint scope; doctor-rule completeness at 34.5%; the scoping mechanism with exactly one real consumer
| `030` | 6 | the `claude_runtime` direct-import binding left in place; `permission_fix.py`'s DSL symbols; four inventory rows registered open and un-drawn
| *TOTAL* | *17* | *1.31 residual items per deliverable*
|===

[NOTE]
====
**The residue was disclosed by the runs, not concealed from them.** Every one of the 17 was
independently reproduced at `2cd1a19c`, and **each was already named in its own run report** as
out-of-scope, deferred, or registered in the coupling inventory. The ingestion pass confirmed the
disclosures rather than catching concealment.

⭐ **That is a genuinely good result and is stated as one.** In `review-apparatus`, 18.1% of gaps were
corrections to the runs' *own reports*; in `truthful-signals`, 16.6%. **In this corpus the ingestion
pass found no false claim in any of the three run reports about what had landed.**

⚠ **With the caveat that carries it:** the sibling figure counts a *checklist sweep's* corrections,
and no such sweep ran here. The honest statement is *"the claims the plans made were true"*, never
*"the plans' reports contain no false statements"* — the second was not tested.

[WARNING]
====
⭐⭐ **The resolution rate is time-dependent, and this corpus is where that became visible. It
qualifies the sibling reports' central discriminator.**

The classifier was validated exactly on *finding counts* for #1330 and #1332 — but its **resolution**
figures disagree with the same retrospectives:

[cols="2,2,2",options="header"]
|===
| PR | Retrospective (at finalize) | This measurement (post-merge)

| `#1330` | 0 of 3 resolved (0.0%) | 3 of 3 resolved
| `#1332` | 3 of 6 resolved (50.0%) | 6 of 6 resolved
|===

Both moved **upward** after the retrospective ran. ⇒ **A thread's `resolved` flag read post-merge is
not the same measurement as resolution-before-merge**, and only the second is evidence about a
merge gate.

⛔ **`review-apparatus`'s "16 of 16, 100% in both lanes" and `truthful-signals`' "local 100%, cloud
57.1%" were both read post-merge from the same flag.** Some unknown share of each is
resolution-after-the-fact. This report cannot quantify that share — n = 2 for the disagreement — but
it can state that the instrument does not measure what the discriminator needs it to, and that the
sibling verdicts inherit the defect.

**This is the strongest methodological finding in this report**, and it argues *against* this
report's own 88.1%, which is likewise post-merge and therefore an upper bound.
====

=== ⭐ Sourcery's decline is lane-caused here, not environmental

The sibling reports found reviewer coverage degraded by an **account-level quota** — environmental,
shared across lanes, and therefore a control that *removed* it as an explanation. **This corpus has a
different cause, and it is attributable to the lane.**

All three plan PRs carry the identical notice: *"your pull request is larger than the review limit of
150000 diff characters."* Measured directly:

[cols="2,2,2,2",options="header"]
|===
| PR | Diff characters | Sourcery limit | Over by

| `#1291` | 194,176 | 150,000 | **1.29×**
| `#1313` | 376,702 | 150,000 | **2.51×**
| `#1319` | 326,371 | 150,000 | **2.18×**
|===

⇒ **The cloud lane's plan sizing deterministically disqualifies one of three reviewers.** This is not
a quota that happened to be exhausted; it is a size threshold the lane exceeds by construction, on
every plan PR, in a corpus where the two authoring PRs — smaller — were reviewed by Sourcery without
incident.

⚠ **PR-Agent was additionally absent on two of three plan PRs** (`#1291`, `#1313`), which `010`'s own
report independently recorded as *"did not run on this PR — no review, no comment, no check run,
across repeated polls."* Cause unestablished; recorded as *absent*, never as *reviewed with no
findings*.

**Reviewer coverage on plan work: 1 of 3 on `#1291` and `#1313`, 2 of 3 on `#1319`.**

=== The run reports' own reviewer figures are not reproducible

[cols="2,3,2",options="header"]
|===
| Plan | Report claims | Fingerprint measurement

| `010` | "11 actionable comments" (+1 on re-review) | 10 findings / 10 threads
| `020` | "Round A — 9 actionable, 6 nitpicks" | 12 findings / 12 threads
| `030` | "23 actionable comments over **10 inline threads**" | 20 findings / **20 threads**
|===

The disagreement runs in **both directions** and is largest on `030`, where the report's thread count

⚠ **This is a measurement disagreement, not a demonstrated falsehood.** The reports are most likely
transcribing CodeRabbit's own *"Actionable comments posted: N"* summary line, which need not equal the
count of inline threads carrying a `v1` fingerprint. The classifier is validated (<<_validation>>);
what it counts is well-defined; **the two instruments are counting different things and neither report
says which it used.** That ambiguity is the finding.

== Adjudication of the three candidate explanations

=== 1. plan-marshall shows its benefit — *NOT ADJUDICABLE in this corpus*

[CAUTION]
====
⛔ **This report cannot speak to candidate 1, and says so rather than gesturing at it.** The
comparison requires a local set, which this epic does not have and which the operator has scoped out.
Its deliverable-fidelity figure is produced by the narrowest of the four instruments and its gap count
does not exist.

Publishing a verdict on candidate 1 from a 3-plan, no-gap-record, no-local-set corpus would be
manufacturing a conclusion out of an absent measurement — the exact failure mode these reports exist
to catch.
====

=== 2. `cloud-plan-lane` is faulty — *SUPPORTED, on three mechanisms, one of them new*

==== (a) ⭐⭐ Plan sizing disqualifies a reviewer, deterministically

**New to this report.** All three plan PRs exceed Sourcery's 150,000-character limit — by 1.29×,
2.51× and 2.18×. The lane authors plans large enough to lose a third of its reviewer roster on every
run, and the two smaller authoring PRs in the same corpus were reviewed normally.

⇒ **A named mechanism with a measured consequence**: 3 of 3 plan PRs declined, 0 of 2 authoring PRs.
This is not the account-quota cause the sibling reports found and correctly classified as
environmental; it is a property of how the lane sizes its units of work.

==== (b) The verification pass is optional, and here it was simply not run

The lane's third billed bucket produced nothing for any of these three plans. **No `verification.md`,
no `gaps.md`, no severity grading.** Whatever the other corpora's gap counts measure, this epic has no
equivalent record — and nothing in the lane required one before the epic was declared complete enough
to hand over.

⇒ The consequence is measurable in this report's own structure: § 1 exists because the measurement
does not.

==== (c) The reports' own reviewer figures are unreproducible

Three for three, in both directions, largest on `030` (10 threads claimed, 20 measured). Neither the
reports nor the lane contract states which instrument produces the number. ⇒ § 5.2.

⚠ **What this corpus does NOT support**, and the sibling reports did: the *self-assessed report*
mechanism. `review-apparatus` found 18.1% of gaps correcting the runs' own paperwork and
`truthful-signals` 16.6%. **The ingestion pass here found no false landing claim in any of the three
reports.** On the narrower question this instrument asked, the reports were accurate.

=== 3. A general Claude Code issue — *WEAKLY SUPPORTED, and one sibling control is broken here*

* **PR-Agent absent on 2 of 3 plan PRs**, with no notice published — `010` polled repeatedly and
  recorded the absence. Cause unestablished; plausibly reviewer-side.
* ⭐ **The sibling reports' account-quota control does NOT apply to this corpus.** Sourcery declined
  here for a *size* reason that is lane-attributable (§ 8.2(a)). A control that eliminated a candidate
  in two sibling reports therefore **does not transfer** — and its absence moves evidence toward
  candidate 2, not toward candidate 3.
* **Squash merges** make per-commit claims uncheckable, as in every sibling corpus.

== Verdict

[IMPORTANT]
====
**Outcome 2, alone — and with an explicit refusal to adjudicate outcome 1.**

What this corpus establishes:

* ⭐⭐ **The lane sizes its plans past a reviewer's hard limit, on every plan PR** (194k / 377k / 326k
  characters against a 150,000 limit). One of three reviewers was disqualified by construction, not by
  chance. **This is the finding unique to this report.**
* **The third billed bucket — verification — never ran**, so the epic has no gap record at all. Its
  absence is not a clean result; it is a missing measurement, and it is why this report is the
  weakest of the four.
* **5 of 42 reviewer findings merged unresolved** (88.1%), against 0 of 16 in `review-apparatus`.
* **`020`'s report shipped its Cost section as an unfilled template placeholder.**

What this corpus establishes in the lane's *favour*, and it is not nothing:

* **12 of 13 deliverables landed as specified; 3 of 3 plans fully landed; no silent descoping in
  any of them.**
* **No false landing claim was found in any run report** — the 17 residual items were all
  self-disclosed by the runs that left them.

⭐⭐ **The methodological finding outranks both, and applies beyond this epic: the resolution rate
every sibling report rests on is read post-merge, and the two PRs where an independent
finalize-time measurement exists both moved upward afterwards (0/3 → 3/3, 3/6 → 6/6).** The
discriminator measures resolution-eventually, not resolution-before-merge. This report's own 88.1% is
subject to the same defect and is an upper bound.

⚠ **The honest limit:** n = 3 plans, 13 deliverables, 0 gap records, no local set, and no record of
the model or effort the runs used. **Four of the method's eleven steps could not be performed.** The
verdict rests on the reviewer measurements and the diff-size finding, both of which are derived from
the PR record rather than from any artifact the lane authored about itself — which is precisely why
they survive the corpus's weaknesses.
====

== What the cloud lane is genuinely good at

Measured, not inferred:

* **Deliverable fidelity is real.** All 13 deliverables were re-derived from the implementing source
  and 12 landed exactly as specified — including the 24-operation ABC count, the fail-closed
  validation paths, and the parameterised dispatch, each checked against code rather than docstrings.
* **Self-disclosure is honest and detailed.** All 17 residual items were already named by the runs
  that left them. `030` disclosed that its own headline — "zero Claude literals" — was narrower than
  the slogan, and registered every literal it found beyond its five clusters rather than fixing or
  dropping them.
* **The verification loop is deep.** `020` ran **16 rounds**, `030` **6 plus 2 external reviews**;
  `030`'s later rounds added security hardening beyond the brief (a `protect-path` refusal set, a
  fail-closed helper applied to all six mutating branches) that is present in the tree today.
* **A regression caught by the run's own loop.** `030`'s round 9 found a segment-wise `.claude/skills`
  construction reintroduced in `configurable_contract.py` — by its own earlier review-fix commit — and
  fixed it.
* **Review findings were engaged with, not absorbed.** `020` accepted 9 of 9 CodeRabbit findings and
  **widened two beyond what was asked**; `030` declined two on the thread with stated reasoning.

== Recommendations

Ordered by the measured consequence each addresses.

. **Bound plan size against the reviewer limits.** 3 of 3 plan PRs exceeded Sourcery's 150,000-character
  threshold; the two smaller authoring PRs did not. A plan that cannot be reviewed by a third of the
  roster is mis-sized, and the limit is a published number the lane can check before it opens the PR.
. ⭐ **Measure reviewer resolution at merge time, not post-hoc — in every report including the three
  already published.** The flag moves upward after merge (0/3 → 3/3, 3/6 → 6/6 on the two PRs where
  both instruments exist). Until a finalize-time figure is captured, no resolution rate in this
  document set is evidence about a merge gate.
. **Make the verification pass non-optional, or stop treating an epic as analysable without it.**
  This epic reached "complete enough to ingest" with bucket 3 never spent, and the result is a report
  that cannot adjudicate its primary question.
. **Fail a run whose report carries an unfilled template placeholder.** `020` shipped
  `_(filled in at close)_` as its entire Cost section.
. **State which instrument a reviewer count came from.** Three reports, three unreproducible figures,
  no stated method.
. **Record the model and effort setting in the run report.** This is the only corpus of the four where
  neither an artifact nor an operator statement exists, and it removes an entire line of argument.
. **Budget the ingestion.** Five sub-agents, three landing analyses, 43 persisted verdicts and nine new
  specs — the recurring, unbudgeted fourth expenditure the sibling reports also name.

== Reproducing this analysis

See xref:analyzis_rules.adoc[the analysis rules] for the instruction set. Every figure was derived by command or
script from the ingested corpus at `.plan/local/orchestrator/multiplattform/archive/`, the landing
records at `../landings/`, and the PR record via
`plan-marshall:tools-integration-ci:ci pr comments --pr-number N`; none was transcribed by hand.

Four of the method's eleven steps were **not performed**, each for a stated reason: Step 3 (gap
classification — no `gaps.md` exists), Step 4 and Step 6 (local comparison — out of scope by operator
instruction), and the cloud half of Step 8 (model/effort — unrecorded in the corpus). The classifier
validation is in <<_validation>>; two divergences from the method's preconditions are recorded in
§ 1.
