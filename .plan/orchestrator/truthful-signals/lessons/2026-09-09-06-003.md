# A declaration-flip's ship ruling drew its safety population from the very declaration under question

## Problem

Plan `pre-push-gate-mutation-coherence` flipped `default:pre-push-quality-gate` from
`mutates_source: false` to `true`. Its deliverable-2 artifact
(`mutation-instrumentation-observation.md`) carried an explicit STOP clause — *stop rather than ship
if the new absorption behaviour is worse than the status quo for a manifest configuration a real plan
can actually reach* — and evaluated it by enumerating the steps that could dirty the tree ahead of
the gate:

> ### The steps ordered below the gate that declare `mutates_source: true`
>
> | Step | order | `mutates_source` | … |
> | `default:finalize-step-sync-baseline` | 3 | `true` | … |
> | `project:finalize-step-lessons-housekeeping` | 4 | `true` | … |

The population predicate is **"declares `mutates_source: true`"**. But the plan's entire subject was
that this declaration can be **false about the step's actual behaviour** — that is why the flip was
being made at all. So the safety analysis filtered its population by exactly the field whose
truthfulness it did not trust, and any step that dirties the tree while declaring `false` was
excluded from the population **by construction**, not by evidence.

## What the exclusion cost, in the same run

That excluded class is not hypothetical. `default:pre-submission-self-review` declares
`mutates_source: false` and is find-only on its ordinary path, but on the `6-finalize` inline-replay
loop-back tier a single-annotation FIX is a source edit. On this very plan's finalize, rounds 2 and 3
left five modified files with no committing owner; the orchestrator committed them by hand as
`426982695` (filed as lesson `2026-09-08-20-001`). That commit moved HEAD out from under every
head-dependent verdict already recorded, which in turn forced a freshness re-establishment
(lesson `2026-09-09-06-002`) and made `review_gate_delta` withhold its verdict with
`exclusion_reason: gate_tree_unsubstantiated`.

The artifact's "Residual window" paragraph even reached for this window and closed it with the same
wrong predicate: *"The only steps in that window are order-4 `lessons-housekeeping` — itself
`mutates_source: true`, so it carries its own item-5f commit — and the gate itself."* A
capability-derived enumeration would have listed `pre-submission-self-review` there and surfaced the
defect before the run hit it.

## Why this is not just "the sweep missed a step"

The ruling is not wrong because the author overlooked one entry. It is wrong because the **predicate
that generated the entries** is the negation of the plan's own premise. A capability-derived
population (*which steps can leave a dirty tracked path?*) and a declaration-derived population
(*which steps say they will?*) coincide only when every declaration is already true — which is the
conclusion, not an admissible input.

Note that the artifact's other arms are sound precisely because they do NOT do this: arm 4 pins the
gate's non-mutation by asserting on the **constructed argv** at `build.subprocess.run`, and arms 2/3
are a matched pair that rules out a fixture explanation. The declaration-derived step table is the
one place the artifact reasoned from a claim instead of a measurement, and that is the one place it
was wrong.

## Directive

When a plan changes a **declaration field** — `mutates_source`, `head_dependent`, `default_on`,
`lane.class`, `participation_requires_update`, any frontmatter fact a dispatcher routes on:

1. **Derive the safety population by capability, never by the field being flipped.** Ask "which
   elements can produce the effect?", not "which elements declare that they do". For
   `mutates_source` that means enumerating every step reachable on a tier that permits a source edit
   — including the inline-replay loop-back tier, which turns a find-only step into a mutating one
   without touching its declaration.
2. **State the predicate explicitly in the ruling, next to the table it produced.** A table whose
   selection rule is left implicit cannot be checked by a reviewer; the rule is the part that is
   falsifiable, not the rows.
3. **Treat a same-run counterexample as ruling input, not as a separate incident.** This run
   produced its own falsifying instance ~40 minutes after the artifact shipped its SHIP verdict, and
   the artifact was never revisited. A plan whose own finalize exercises the mechanism it changed is
   running a live arm; fold that arm's observation — especially a negative one — back into the
   observation artifact before the plan closes.
