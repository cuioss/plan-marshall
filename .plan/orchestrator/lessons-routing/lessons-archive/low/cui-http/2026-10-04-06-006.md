# pre-push-quality-gate degrades in a Maven project with no default-scope module-tests canonical

# cui-http has no default-scope module-tests canonical, so pre-push-quality-gate's module-tests arm degrades

## What happened

On PLAN-10 (#256) and again on PLAN-12 (#260) the pre-push-quality-gate reported its module-tests arm DEGRADED ("no module-tests canonical resolves at default scope"), while the whole-tree `verify -Ppre-commit` ran the full suite green (12512 and 12541 tests).

## Rule (cui-http)

Register a default-scope module-tests canonical for cui-http (e.g. `test -pl cui-http-core -am`) so the divergence arm can run, or record explicitly that the whole-tree quality gate supersedes it.

## Scope note

The marketplace half (gate scope derived from marketplace bundles; no `resolve-test-scope` verb in build-maven) was routed to plan-marshall epic `truthful-signals` on 2026-10-05 and is not tracked here.
