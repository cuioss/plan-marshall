## Context

Finalize cost ~equal to execute, driven by gate re-firing (5 head-dependent steps, 20
firings): HEAD advanced for reasons unrelated to what the gates read. The
verdict-currency classifier returns `invalidated` on any advance because no step
declares a `verdict_inputs` surface — with nothing to compare against, one safe answer
every time.

## Proposed action

HEAD delta: `current` when disjoint, `invalidated` only on intersection. Opt-in (no
declaration ⇒ today's unconditional invalidate). Complements 06-10-001's staleness
assertion (that covers steps that do NOT re-fire; this covers comparing for steps that do).
