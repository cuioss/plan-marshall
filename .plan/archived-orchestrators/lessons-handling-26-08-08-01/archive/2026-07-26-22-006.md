# architecture enrich writes fail the phases-2/3/4 clean-main contract assertion

## Observation

Plan `orchestration-inbox-channel` halted at the phases-2/3/4 **post-dispatch contract assertion** (`git -C . status --porcelain` must be empty) after phase-4-plan. The dirty files were not source drift:

```
 M .plan/project-architecture/default/enriched.json
 M .plan/project-architecture/plan-marshall/enriched.json
```

They were `architecture enrich` writes — five corpus-derived `best_practices` entries appended by a phase that legitimately carries `plan-marshall:manage-architecture` in its skill set.

## Root cause

The assertion's own rationale states it is safe because "plan-workspace writes under `.plan/local/**` are untracked, so they never appear in porcelain output — only a stray main-tree edit does." That premise is incomplete. The architecture knowledge store lives at `.plan/project-architecture/**`, which **is** tracked. So a sanctioned operation (`architecture enrich`) performed by a phase that was deliberately given the enriching skill structurally fails a hard gate that is documented as catching rogue source edits.

The three assertion sites share the defect:

- `plan-marshall/workflow/planning.md` § Post-dispatch contract assertion (phase-2-refine)
- `plan-marshall/workflow/planning-outline.md` § Post-dispatch contract assertion (phase-3-outline)
- `plan-marshall/workflow/planning-outline.md` Step 4b § Post-dispatch contract assertion (phase-4-plan)

Each restates the same untracked-therefore-invisible premise, and each names only `.plan/marshal.json` as a recovery case.

## Why it matters

The failure mode is a **confident signal hiding a caveat** — the theme of the `truthful-signals` epic. Two bad outcomes are both live today:

1. The orchestrator reports `plan_contract_violation` with `display_detail: "plan dispatched edits to main checkout"` — factually wrong about what happened, and alarming for what is a benign enrichment.
2. Worse, the natural repair (revert to clear the gate) **discards real learning** the enrichment just derived. On this run that meant losing five corpus-wide observations about q-gate resolution patterns. They are re-derivable, but nothing in the flow says so, and nothing routes them anywhere durable first.

## Directive

Decide the intended contract and make the machinery match it — do not add a runtime guard that special-cases the path string at each of the three sites.

Either:

(a) **`architecture enrich` is not permitted in phases 2-4.** Then the phases must not carry `manage-architecture` write capability, and the assertion is already correct — but the skill sets must be narrowed so the operation is unreachable rather than merely punished after the fact.

(b) **`architecture enrich` is permitted.** Then `.plan/project-architecture/**` belongs in the assertion's allowed set at all three sites, alongside the existing `.plan/local/plans/{plan_id}/**` carve-out, with the rationale stated once and cross-referenced rather than restated three times.

Whichever is chosen, correct the assertion's stated premise: "plan-workspace writes under `.plan/local/**` are untracked" is true but not sufficient, because `.plan/project-architecture/**` is tracked and is written by a sanctioned verb.

Also verify whether the same premise appears at the phase-5/phase-6 porcelain checks.
