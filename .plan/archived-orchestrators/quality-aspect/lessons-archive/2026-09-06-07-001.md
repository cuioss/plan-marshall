# Classify review_body actionable content by residual text, not the status line

## Context

On PR #1422 (plan `domain-post-plan-narrow`) the `pr-comment` findings store held 17 CodeRabbit records: 11 `inline`, 5 `review_body`, 1 `issue_comment`. All five `review_body` records were bucketed `meta` by `review_body_summary_patterns`, because each opens with the registry-declared status line `Actionable comments posted: N`.

That bucketing was correct for exactly one of the five. The other four carried real findings *behind* the status line — a nitpick to derive `_ALL_FOUR` from `_DOMAINS_CONFIG['skill_domains']`, an outside-diff Major to fail closed when a resolved task cannot be evaluated, two outside-diff findings plus a nitpick on error-code list sync, and a nitpick to derive the mirror population from the scan. Every one of those six items was triaged FIX and landed.

CodeRabbit's real actionable yield was therefore 11 inline + 6 review-body-carried = 17 items, against a measured `actionable_count` of 11. The instrument undercounts the reviewer by 35% on this PR.

## Root cause

The classifier keys on the PRESENCE of the opening status line rather than on whether content survives it. GitHub forces a reviewer's outside-diff findings into the review body, so a reviewer that reports where the platform makes it report is systematically scored as having posted meta. The defect is in the measuring instrument, not in the reviewer.

This matters beyond one number: `actionable_count` feeds per-reviewer yield comparison, and yield comparison is the stated instrument for roster decisions (`review_completeness deficit` accumulation). An instrument that under-credits whichever reviewer posts outside the diff will, over enough PRs, recommend dropping the wrong bot.

## Proposed action

Change `review_body_summary_patterns` classification to a two-step test:

1. Match and STRIP the registry-declared status-summary prefix (`Actionable comments posted: N` and siblings).
2. Bucket on what remains. A record whose residue is empty is `meta`; a record carrying nitpicks, outside-diff findings, or any other actionable body is `actionable`, and the status line is treated as an envelope rather than as the content.

Do not simply widen the meta patterns — that moves the error in the same direction.

## Evidence

- aspect: request_result_alignment — the plan's own `review-retrospective.md` records the per-record breakdown and the "undercounts by 35%" verdict as recommendation 1.
- aspect: script_failure_analysis — the four under-credited records are named individually (`fe0db8`, `effd2f`, `8f4d55`, `da6d9a`) with their dispositions.
- Store evidence: `artifacts/findings/pr-comment.jsonl`, 17 records, `findings_store_state: present`; per-`(author, kind)` split `inline 11 / review_body 5 / issue_comment 1`.
