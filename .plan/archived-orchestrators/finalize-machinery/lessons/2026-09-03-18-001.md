## What happened

Whole-tree verify failed on a pollution-guard ERROR naming an innocent test —
`merge.lock` belonged to a concurrent sibling plan. Green suite; re-run with the lock in
the baseline snapshot passed. Attribution randomly wrong (widest-window test blamed),
reads as scattered flakiness, vanishes on re-run — inviting misclassification while the
unsound attribution persists.

## How to apply

Scope the snapshot to the sandbox, or ignore entries attributable to another live plan
(`merge.lock` records `holder_plan_id`).
