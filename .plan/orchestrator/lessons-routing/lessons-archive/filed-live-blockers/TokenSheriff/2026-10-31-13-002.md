# Pre-merge HEAD binding voids every gate on any late commit; settle the final tree first and build the barrier prompt from its own labels

## Problem

The pre-merge HEAD-binding check refuses with `merge_candidate_not_gated` whenever HEAD moved after the head-dependent gates ran. Four related friction points: PLAN-01 (two late commits, a formatting rewrite and a docs-only ADR commit; six gates re-ran), PLAN-02 (`finalize-step-sync-baseline` stamped an old `head_at_completion`, was skipped on re-entry as not head-dependent, but the gate derives head-dependence from that stamp; the forced rebase voided every verdict), PLAN-06 (a test-only commit after the gated head; the operator's earlier re-run waivers were accepted by the step records but refused at merge, so the security audit and functional review re-ran about three hours later), and, at the same barrier on PLAN-01, the grant verb refused a paraphrased answer, "Accept gap and merge", because it accepts only the barrier's own label "Merge anyway (record reason)").

## Solution

Settle the final tree before the head-dependent gates run: formatting rewrites and artifact commits (ADR drafts) land first. A docs-only or test-only commit still voids every HEAD-bound record, so do not add one late without planning the re-run. Re-run `finalize-step-sync-baseline` explicitly when main advanced before the gate fires. Do not rely on an operator waiver of a re-run: confirm the merge gate accepts it. Build the operator prompt from the option labels the barrier returns, word for word. Tooling fixes to consider: one source for the skip rule and the gate's head-dependence, a cheap re-attestation path for docs-only deltas, and a ready-made prompt envelope carrying the barrier's labels.

## Impact

A late, expensive gate refusal that forces a rebase and a full verdict re-run, often hours after the operator thought the plan was done.
