# Verdict-currency re-fires full reviews on every HEAD advance because review steps declare no verdict_inputs

The verdict-currency classifier treats any HEAD advance as stale because the review steps declare no `verdict_inputs`; review steps should declare them (the footprint paths and the diff against the merge base), and the classifier should keep a verdict current when a HEAD advance leaves the plan's own diff unchanged, as a pure rebase onto unrelated upstream commits does.

## What happened

In plan validation-public-seams (PR #899), a branch-cleanup rebase onto an unrelated upstream commit (c23e2a85, #898) changed no plan file, yet it re-fired functional-review and code-review. The cost was two full review rounds plus the gate, CI and triage that follow them. The same run recorded a non-zero exit from `plan-marshall:phase-6-finalize:verdict_currency` itself (signal-scan class script_failure); its argv and error are in the plan work log and the cause was not classified.

## Rule

Declare `verdict_inputs` on the review steps; treat a HEAD advance that keeps the plan's own diff identical as verdict-current. Investigate the verdict_currency script_failure together with this candidate, since both concern the same script.
