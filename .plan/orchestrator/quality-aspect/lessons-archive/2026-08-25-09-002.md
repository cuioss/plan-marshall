# Stamp returned_with_findings when a self-review round loops back with findings

## Context

The 6-finalize dispatch-boundary ledger records 13 rows. Ten are `step_complete`; three are `error`, carrying 679,275 tokens — 19% of the phase's whole 3.57M spend. All three `error` rows are `pre-submission-self-review` rounds that examined their surface, filed findings, and had those findings fixed: `status.json` shows `prior_firings: [failed, failed, failed]` before the fourth round closed `done`, and decision `fae1f5` records round 2's findings driving a convergent narrowing fix. Round 4 examined 301 candidates.

`returned_with_findings_count` is `0` in every phase of this plan.

`logging-gap-analysis.md` defines `error_total_tokens` as the spend on dispatches that are \"genuinely non-productive: they raised a fatal `error` and returned nothing (findings-bearing loop-backs are now stamped `returned_with_findings`, not `error`, so what remains under `error` is genuine terminal waste). This is the figure a reader acts on.\"

A reader acting on this plan's figure would conclude 679K tokens bought zero detection. The opposite is true: those rounds are where the run's real defects were caught, including a correctness bug in `_raw_mentions_module` that demoted `not_derivable` to `unclaimed` — the exact merge the tool exists to prevent.

## Root cause

`returned_with_findings` is stamped by the finalize dispatcher only when a dispatched step's `mark-step-done` recorded `outcome: loop_back`. `pre-submission-self-review` stamps `outcome: failed` on a findings-bearing round. The productive-loop-back cause is therefore structurally unreachable for the highest-re-firing step in the finalize lane, and every one of its productive rounds falls through to `error`.

## Proposed action

Make a findings-bearing self-review round stamp `outcome: loop_back` rather than `failed`, so the dispatcher reaches `returned_with_findings`. Reserve `failed` / `error` for a round that could not complete its examination.

Keep the two distinguishable at the step level too: `prior_firings: [failed, failed, failed]` currently cannot tell \"found defects\" from \"could not look\", which is the same conflation one tier up.

## Evidence

- aspect: logging_gap_analysis — 3 `error` rows / 679,275 tokens; `returned_with_findings` 0 plan-wide
- aspect: log_analysis — `6-finalize.error_total_tokens: 679275`, `returned_with_findings_count: 0`, `retryable_total_tokens: 0`
- aspect: plan_efficiency — the same 679K is 19% of the dominant phase
- decision `fae1f5` — round-2 findings are real and drove a convergent fix, not a failure

## Recurrence — 2026-09-04, plan `domain-glob-seeding` (PR #1406)

**Third independent instance, and the cleanest demonstration of the metric corruption.** The
6-finalize dispatch-boundary ledger holds 9 rows: 7 `step_complete`, 1 `returned_with_findings`,
1 `error`. The single `error` row carries **296,980 tokens — 100% of the plan's
`error_total_tokens`** — and it is the `pre-submission-self-review` round that found 4 real
`contract_drift` findings (closed enumerations this plan's own change invalidated), all of which
were fixed at `047b723`. Round 2 was clean.

This run makes the disagreement legible in one plan, because the *other* review-shaped step
behaved correctly beside it: `plan-marshall:automatic-review` recorded
`prior_firings: [{outcome: loop_back, loop_back_target: 6-finalize}]` and was duly stamped
`returned_with_findings`. Two review steps, one phase, two different outcome vocabularies for
the identical situation — found findings, looped back, fixed, re-fired clean.

Also newly identified: the mismatch is **prescribed by the step's own authoritative doc**, not
merely emitted by a call site. `phase-6-finalize/workflow/pre-submission-self-review.md` states
it at three places — line 35 ("a non-empty findings list records `outcome=failed` and halts the
phase"), Step 4 Branch B (line 422, the literal `--outcome failed` invocation), and the D5
paragraph at line 442 ("D5 introduces NO new `mark-step-done` outcome: a self-seeding round
still has findings, so it records `--outcome failed`"). The stamping rule it must meet is
`phase-6-finalize/SKILL.md` line 1110, which keys `returned_with_findings` on `outcome: loop_back`.

So the fix is a **documentation change in three sites plus one call site**, not a one-line
outcome swap — and any fix must keep the gating behaviour (`failed` halts the phase) that
line 35 relies on while still reaching the productive-loop-back cause. `loop_back` with
`--loop-back-target 6-finalize` gives exactly that: the dispatcher's inline-replay path
re-fires the step, which is what the run already does.

## Recurrence — 2026-09-06, plan `domain-post-plan-narrow` (PR #1422)

**Fourth independent instance, now PARTIALLY fixed — and the partial fix is the finding.**
`pre-submission-self-review` recorded `firing_count: 7` with
`prior_firings: [loop_back, failed, loop_back, done, loop_back, loop_back]`. Four rounds
stamped `loop_back` correctly; **one stamped `failed`**, in the same run, for the same
situation.

That mixed vocabulary within a single step's own firing history is new information. The
previous three instances showed the step stamping `failed` uniformly, which reads as an
unapplied fix. Here the correct outcome is reachable and is used four times out of five, so
what remains is a residual path — not a missing capability. Whatever branch produced the
lone `failed` was not covered by the correction the earlier instances prompted.

The downstream cost is also newly visible at the round level rather than only in the token
figure: the `failed` round denied the next round its delta anchor, so the following round
could not scope itself to what had changed since the last examination and re-derived from a
wider base.

The dispatch ledger for this run is otherwise healthy — `6-finalize` recorded 14 rows with
`returned_with_findings_count: 5`, `error_total_tokens: 0` — which is precisely why the
single `failed` step-outcome is now the whole of the residue. Fix the remaining branch, and
add a guard asserting that a self-review round with a non-empty findings list can never
record `failed`.
