# The finalize gates cannot evidence their own results before they run

## Problem

Q-Gate finding a46d0c (`unevidenced_criterion at D26.C1`, round 2): the criterion "all three gates green and recorded in the PR body" was unevidenced because verify had not been re-run after two fix commits and the Sonar PR-head count is produced by the finalize gates themselves. The operator accepted it.

## Solution

A deliverable whose criterion is "the finalize gates pass" is circular when the functional review runs inside those gates. Such criteria should be marked as evidenced by the finalize step results, not reviewed as part of the deliverable.

## Provenance

Candidate lesson from plan plan-25-epic-leftover-sweep (epic quality-report), inbox message 018; source: qgate 6-finalize a46d0c (functional-review, round 2, accepted). Same circularity as 002 (criterion needing the PR) and 001-class criteria.

## Recurrence: a criterion whose evidence is the PR body (plan-27-test-size-guideline-restore, PR #887)

The verifier refused a verdict (`verdict_refused`, finding de8a4b) because the author graded D1.C5 `met` although its PR-body clause could not hold yet: no PR body existed at the pre-push functional review. A criterion whose evidence exists only after the PR is created cannot be graded `met` at that step. The step looped back once before the operator closed it. Same cause, different artifact: the circular evidence here is the PR body, not the gate results.
