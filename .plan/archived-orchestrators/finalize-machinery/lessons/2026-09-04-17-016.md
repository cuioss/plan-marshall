# 2026-09-04-17-016 — A force-push defeats the review-currency guard, because it orders timestamps instead of comparing the SHA the bot says it reviewed

component: plan-marshall:workflow-integration-github
category: bug
status: moved-to-epic (corpus original retired 2026-09-17: completely_covered by PR #1510)
source: corpus lesson 2026-09-04-17-016, moved into finalize-machinery per operator direction

## The defect

The currency test credits a bot when the comment does not demonstrably predate the
merge candidate — implemented as timestamp ordering (comment latest timestamp vs
candidate committer date). Under rebase + force-push, a review of the superseded tree
posted after the rewrite satisfies "later than the candidate" and is credited as
current on a required bot. Observed on the flag's own introducing PR: CodeRabbit
reviewed range `bf1b7ed67..9ede8d9cb` (pre-force-push head); comment edit_term later
than candidate committer date → credited `participated`.

## Second-order failure

Stored findings recorded `reviewed_commit_sha` as the merge candidate at fetch time,
not the SHA the bot named — so the re-review trigger reads already-current and the
staleness is invisible.

## Remedy

Compare the SHA the bot names against the merge candidate (parseable range sentence);
where no SHA is stated, verdict `undecidable` (fail-closed). Persist the bot-named SHA.
General hunting rule: look for the proxy (timestamp for SHA, file list for diff, stale
snapshot for live config).

## Coverage (finalize-machinery)

Remedy shipped by PLAN-03 / PR #1510: `_github_pr.py` compares bot-claimed reviewed
SHA against the candidate HEAD. Corpus original retired as completely_covered.
