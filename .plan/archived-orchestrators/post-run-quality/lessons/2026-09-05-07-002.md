# termination_cause=error is stamped on findings-producing steps, inflating the waste figure

## Context

Plan `shipped-guards-assume-the-meta-projects-own-layout` recorded 24 dispatch-boundary rows in `6-finalize`. Eleven of them carry `termination_cause: error` and, between them, 2,323,435 tokens — 39% of the phase and 24% of the whole plan.

All eleven are `pre-submission-self-review` firings. Every one of them ran, examined its surface, filed real findings, and those findings were fixed: the round loop produced 26 findings across 12 rounds and resolved all of them. Not one of the eleven "examined nothing and returned nothing".

That phrase is not incidental. `plan-retrospective/references/logging-gap-analysis.md` defines `error_total_tokens` as "the spend on dispatches whose terminal state is **genuinely non-productive**: they raised a fatal `error` and returned nothing", and tells the reader this "is the figure a reader acts on". On this plan that figure is 2.32M tokens of productive detection spend, presented as pure waste.

## Root cause

`phase-6-finalize` stamps `returned_with_findings` only when the step's `mark-step-done` recorded `outcome: loop_back` (SKILL.md Step 3 item 5c). `pre-submission-self-review` does not loop back — it records `outcome: failed` and the orchestrator re-fires it in place.

`DISPATCH_TERMINATION_CAUSES` has no value for *"the step ran, examined its surface, filed findings, and returned failed"*. The stamping rule follows the documented mapping correctly; the vocabulary simply has no admissible landing site for the most common non-completion in finalize, so it falls into `error`.

The consequence is directional, not merely imprecise. `error_total_tokens` is the one field a cost reader is instructed to act on, and it is inflated by exactly the spend that bought the most detection. A remedy driven by it would cap or triage the wrong dispatches.

## Proposed action

Either:

1. Add a `returned_with_findings`-class value that a `failed` outcome carrying findings maps to (e.g. reuse `returned_with_findings` and widen the stamping rule in `phase-6-finalize/SKILL.md` item 5c from `outcome == loop_back` to `outcome in {loop_back, failed} AND the step filed >= 1 finding`); or
2. Keep `error` for the mapping but split the reported sum, so `error_total_tokens` covers only dispatches that filed zero findings and a sibling field carries findings-producing failures.

Whichever is chosen, `logging-gap-analysis.md`'s definition of `error_total_tokens` must be corrected in the same change — it currently asserts a property ("returned nothing") that the stamping rule does not guarantee.

## Impact

Any consumer reading `error_total_tokens` as waste — a cost cap, a triage rule, a cross-plan roll-up — over-counts on every plan whose finalize steps fail productively. On this plan the over-count is 2.32M tokens on a 9.64M-token plan.
