# A refutation resting on a universally-quantified premise must enumerate the population before asserting it

## The rule

A refutation that rests on a universally-quantified premise — "*every* X already satisfies Y" —
must **enumerate the population before asserting it**. Checking two instances and generalizing is a
sampling error, and committing it *while rejecting a finding* is the direction that leaves no trace.

⛔ **A wrongly-accepted finding produces a visible no-op fix. A wrongly-refuted one produces
silence.** That asymmetry is why this needs a mechanical check rather than care.

Second rule, same class: when a disposition cites a prior decision as authority, **the citation must
be read, not recalled.**

## Where it came from

PR #1074. CodeRabbit raised finding `cd99e7` — stale count-prose at
`extension-api/standards/ext-point-derivation-resolver.md:3`, where a cardinality claim ("three
resolvers … two from Axis-A and one from Axis-B") sits apart from its enumeration, which is behind a
cross-reference.

Triage **declined** it on the premise *"every count already sits adjacent to its own enumeration, so
the enumerate-exactly rule is satisfied."* That premise is true for the two sibling sites
(`extension-architecture.adoc:28`, `ext-point-derivation-resolver.md:155`) and **false for line 3** —
the one it did not check.

The compounding error: the disposition named q-gate resolution `817899` as the authority requiring
that wording. `817899`'s own recorded resolution prescribes the **opposite** — *"a roster-free
replacement so a fourth resolver needs no header edit"*. **The cited authority argued against the
refutation it was cited to support**, and its text was available, resolved earlier in the same plan.

Honest scoring of that PR's 6 actionable findings was therefore **4 confirmed / 1 soundly refuted /
1 refuted-but-substantially-correct**, not the 4/2 recorded. **The defect shipped.**

## Why it generalises

This is the same shape as "a reviewer's list of call sites is a SAMPLE, not an enumeration" — a
reviewer named 3 `write_status` callers where 14 existed. Here the sampling error ran in the
*opposite* direction: not under-counting a defect population, but over-claiming a *compliance*
population in order to dismiss one.

⭐ Both directions share one fix: **derive the population**. An asserted absence ("nothing else is
affected") needs exactly the same derivation as an asserted presence.

## Checkable form

Before recording a DECLINE whose reasoning contains *every*, *all*, *none*, *only*, or *always*:

1. Name the population the quantifier ranges over.
2. Enumerate it — mechanically, not by recall.
3. If the enumeration is not feasible, the disposition is **not a refutation** — downgrade it to
   "unconfirmed" rather than declining.
4. If the reasoning cites a prior decision, open that decision and quote it.
