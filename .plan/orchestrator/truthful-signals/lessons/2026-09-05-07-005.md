# record-dispatch-boundary is called without its four context-load flags, so no plan has a billing cost

## Context

`manage-metrics record-dispatch-boundary` accepts four optional context-load flags — `--input-tokens`, `--output-tokens`, `--cache-read-input-tokens`, `--cache-creation-input-tokens` — and writes the literal `unmeasured` into each column the caller omits, so that "no measurement was passed" stays distinguishable from "zero context was loaded".

On plan `shipped-guards-assume-the-meta-projects-own-layout` the distinction did its job and reported the bad news honestly:

```
context_position_cost:
  total_rows: 29
  measured_rows: 0
  unmeasured_rows: 29
  by_phase: 4-plan 1/0, 5-execute 4/0, 6-finalize 24/0
  position_multiple: unmeasured
```

Every dispatch row across all three dispatching phases was recorded with all four columns unmeasured. Downstream, `manage-metrics generate` returned `totals_billing_weighted_total: 0` with `totals_billing_weighted_total_population_count: 0`, and `metrics.md` rendered the `Billing (cost)` column as `-` for every phase and for the Total.

This plan consumed 9,644,036 dispatched tokens over 24h35m wall clock — the most expensive plan on record for this project — and produced **no purchase-cost figure at all**.

## Root cause

The four flags are optional on the recorder and no dispatch call site passes them. The dispatched agent's four-field `message.usage` view exists at termination (that is what `enrich` later walks out of the transcripts), but the dispatcher never forwards it into the boundary row.

`enrich` can partially compensate per phase by walking subagent transcripts — but it did not fire here either, so nothing filled the gap. The per-dispatch cut, which is the one that would say *which* dispatch was expensive to buy, has no other source.

This matters more than an ordinary metrics gap because token reduction is this project's stated first priority, and roughly three quarters of billing weight is context re-read (`cache_read_input_tokens`) rather than generation. The dispatched-token total this plan does have measures how much work was done; it does not measure what the work cost. On a plan with 15 firings of a single step, those two diverge sharply and only the missing measure would show it.

## Proposed action

Have the finalize and execute dispatchers forward the returned agent's four `message.usage` fields into `record-dispatch-boundary` alongside the existing `--total-tokens` / `--tool-uses` / `--duration-ms`. The values are already in hand at the point the existing flags are read; the call simply drops them.

Add a retrospective check that flags `measured_rows == 0` over a non-empty `total_rows` as a warning, so a plan that produced no cost figure says so at finalize rather than being discovered afterwards.

## Impact

No plan currently produces a per-dispatch billing-weighted cost. Every cost-reduction decision is therefore made against the dispatched-work total, which is the wrong measure for the question being asked, and no cross-plan cost trend can be built at all.

## Evidence

- aspect: log_analysis — `context_position_cost.measured_rows: 0` of `total_rows: 29`
- `manage-metrics generate` return — `totals_billing_weighted_total: 0`, `totals_billing_weighted_total_population_count: 0`
- `metrics.md` Phase Breakdown — `Billing (cost)` is `-` on all six phase rows and on the Total
- aspect: plan_efficiency — `billing_weighted_total: unmeasured`, plan totals 9,644,036 tokens / 24h35m

## Recurrence — 2026-09-07 (test-suite-anti-vacuity)

Still live, and now on a plan 70% more expensive than the one that first filed
it. This recurrence is positive evidence the defect has NOT been fixed, not a
restatement of the original.

### Context

`test-suite-anti-vacuity` (PR #1443, merged `33c8140f3`) recorded **45
dispatch-boundary rows across three phases** — `4-plan` 1, `5-execute` 19,
`6-finalize` 25 — and every one of the four context-load columns on every row
reads `unmeasured`:

```
context_position_cost:
  total_rows: 45
  measured_rows: 0
  unmeasured_rows: 45
  by_phase: 4-plan 1/0, 5-execute 19/0, 6-finalize 25/0
  position_multiple: unmeasured
```

`manage-metrics generate` returned `totals_billing_weighted_total: 0` with
`totals_billing_weighted_total_population_count: 0`; `metrics.md` renders
`Billing (cost)` as `-` on every row and on the Total. The plan consumed
**16,371,072 dispatched tokens over 40h53m** and produced no purchase-cost
figure — a larger, longer plan than the original incident, with the same total
absence of cost data.

### What this recurrence adds

The original lesson argued from principle that dispatched-work total and
purchase cost diverge on a plan with many firings of one step. This run supplies
the case: six head-bound finalize steps fired 44 times, `ci-verify` alone 9
times and `pre-push-quality-gate` 8, with 41 of the 44 returning green. Those
re-fires re-read a large context each time and produced almost nothing, which is
precisely the shape that inflates `cache_read_input_tokens` while leaving the
dispatched-work total looking proportionate. The measure that would have shown
it is the one that is missing.

A second, independent attribution gap compounds it on the same rows: six
`harness_cancellation` rows in `5-execute` were each recorded with
`total_tokens: 0`, so `retryable_total_tokens` reports `0` over a population of
six real cancellations. Between the two, neither the *purchase* cost of a
dispatch nor the *wasted* cost of a cancelled one is recoverable from this
plan's ledgers.

### Evidence

- aspect: log_analysis — `context_position_cost.measured_rows: 0` of `total_rows: 45`
- `manage-metrics generate` return — `totals_billing_weighted_total: 0`, `totals_billing_weighted_total_population_count: 0`
- aspect: plan_efficiency — plan totals 16,371,072 tokens / 40h53m, `billing_weighted_total: 0` at population_count 0
- aspect: logging_gap_analysis — `retryable_total_tokens: 0` over 6 `harness_cancellation` rows each recorded at 0 tokens

## Recurrence — 2026-09-08 (pre-push-gate-mutation-coherence)

Third consecutive recurrence, and the first on a plan small enough to rule out
scale as the cause.

### Context

`pre-push-gate-mutation-coherence` (PR #1454, merged `f24b19a51`) is an 8-file,
3-task, 2-deliverable bug fix — an order of magnitude smaller than the two plans
above — and it reproduces the defect exactly:

```
context_position_cost:
  total_rows: 15
  measured_rows: 0
  unmeasured_rows: 15
  by_phase: 4-plan 1/0, 5-execute 3/0, 6-finalize 11/0
  position_multiple: unmeasured
```

`manage-metrics generate` returned `totals_billing_weighted_total: 0` with
`totals_billing_weighted_total_population_count: 0`; `metrics.md` renders
`Billing (cost)` as `-` on every row and on the Total.

### What this recurrence adds

**`enrich` never ran either.** The original lesson noted that `enrich` "can
partially compensate per phase by walking subagent transcripts — but it did not
fire here either". That is now three for three, which makes the compensating path
theoretical rather than a fallback: no observed plan has had its billing figure
recovered by it. The plan's `session_id` was captured and stored
(`status.metadata.session_ids`), so the input `enrich` needs was present and
unused.

**The missing measure is exactly what this plan needed.** The plan consumed
4,679,693 dispatched tokens — **3.6× its `single_module + bug_fix` ERROR anchor of
1.3M** — with `error_total_tokens: 0` and `retryable_total_tokens: 0`. There was no
wasted dispatch and no failed dispatch to blame, so the dispatched-work total
cannot explain where the overrun went. `6-finalize` alone is 55.3% of the total
across 11 dispatches, and `pre-submission-self-review` fired three times over
substantially the same tree. Whether that is expensive *work* or expensive
*context re-read* is precisely the `cache_read_input_tokens` question, and it is
unanswerable for this plan.

**The proposed retrospective check would have fired here.** The original lesson
proposed "a retrospective check that flags `measured_rows == 0` over a non-empty
`total_rows` as a warning". No such check exists yet; this retrospective raised it
by hand, as the previous two did.

- aspect: log_analysis — `context_position_cost.measured_rows: 0` of `total_rows: 15`
- `manage-metrics generate` return — `totals_billing_weighted_total: 0`, `totals_billing_weighted_total_population_count: 0`
