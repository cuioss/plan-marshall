# Decide test-scope guards up front on docs-reconciliation plan specs

A docs-only plan spec (no .java edits) correcting hand-maintained inventories drew a review demand for build-time guards so the lists cannot drift. The operator overrode the spec mid-run to allow test-scope Java; the new tests then drew their own CodeQL findings and cost another loop-back round.

## Solution

When staging a docs-reconciliation plan spec, decide up front whether test-scope guards for hand-maintained doc lists are in scope: allow `src/test/**` explicitly, or route the guards to a sibling plan. Never leave a blanket no-.java rule to be overridden mid-run.

## Impact

One avoided mid-run scope override plus its review round per occurrence.
