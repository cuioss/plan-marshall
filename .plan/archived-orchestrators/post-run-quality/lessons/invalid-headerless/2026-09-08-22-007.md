# A precondition keyed on the line whose absence is the defect can never fire

## Context

`plan-retrospective`'s `RE_ENTRY_COVERAGE` rule
(`references/logging-gap-analysis.md`) is precondition-guarded:

> **Precondition**: at least one `[STATUS] (plan-marshall:phase-5-execute)
> Re-entering execute phase` entry exists in `work.log` ... Plans without any
> `Re-entering` line skip this rule entirely.

The rule's purpose is to detect a phase that re-entered without logging the
re-entry. Its precondition is the presence of a re-entry log line. So the rule
runs when re-entry logging partially works, and skips silently when it does not
work at all — which is the worse defect and the one it exists to catch.

On `pre-push-gate-mutation-coherence` this was not hypothetical. `analyze-logs`
reported `dispatch_clustering: inferred_dispatches 1, starting_markers 1,
re_entering_markers 0`, so the rule skipped. An **independent** source contradicts
that clean skip: `work/metrics-dispatch-boundaries-5-execute.toon` records
**three** dispatch terminations for the phase (2 × `voluntary_checkpoint`, 1 ×
`clean_exit_queue_empty`). The phase was re-entered at least twice and logged the
re-entry zero times, and the rule designed to say so said nothing.

## Root cause

The precondition is drawn from the same signal the rule measures. A guard whose
population is defined by the presence of the artefact it audits is vacuous at the
limit: coverage 0/N and coverage 0/0 are indistinguishable to it, and only the
first is a defect.

The precondition was added for a real reason — not to false-positive on plans that
predate the `Re-entering` marker — but backwards-compatibility was bought by
making the rule blind to total absence rather than by dating the population.

## Proposed action

Re-key the precondition on a signal **independent of the artefact being audited**:

1. Use the dispatch-boundary ledger as the population source.
   `work/metrics-dispatch-boundaries-{phase}.toon` records one row per dispatch
   termination and is written by a different call site than the work-log marker.
   When it holds `N > 1` rows for `5-execute`, at least `N-1` re-entries occurred
   and the expected `Re-entering` count is known without reading a `Re-entering`
   line.
2. Report the three states distinctly: `measured` (ledger present, counts
   compared), `unmeasurable` (no ledger — a genuinely older plan), and the new
   `absent` case (ledger says `N > 1`, zero markers) which is the `error` the rule
   was built for.
3. Apply the same read to every other precondition-guarded rule in that reference
   whose precondition names its own audited marker. `OUTCOME_COVERAGE` has the
   identical shape — its precondition is the presence of an `[OUTCOME]` line — and
   passed here only because outcome pairing happened to be complete (3/3).
