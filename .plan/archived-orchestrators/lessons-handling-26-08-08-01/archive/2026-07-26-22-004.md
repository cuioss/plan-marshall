# Component assessments and sonar scan summaries are counted as unresolved pending findings: 310 of 1519 audit rows are permanently pending by construction, inflating the genuine-signal count by 70%

## Observed

Surfaced by the `quality-chain` check of the full-corpus archived-plan retrospective audit
(115 plans, 2026-07-26). The check reported `finding_genuine_signal_count: 442` over 1,519
per-finding rows. Of those, **310 rows are `resolution=pending`, and every one is stamped
`genuine`** ("unresolved chain debt"). Their actual sources:

| Source file | Pending rows | What the record actually is |
|-------------|-------------:|-----------------------------|
| `assessments.jsonl` | 267 | phase-3-outline component assessments (CERTAIN_INCLUDE / EXCLUDE decisions) |
| `sonar-scan-summary.jsonl` | 41 | per-scan roll-up summaries |
| `improvement.jsonl` / `anti-pattern.jsonl` | 2 | genuine findings, legitimately pending |

**308 of the 310 carry an empty title.** They are decision/summary records, not findings awaiting a
fix. They have no resolution field to fill, so they are `pending` **by construction and forever** —
the signal can never be discharged, no matter how healthy the plan.

`checks/quality-chain.md:25` maps `assessments.jsonl` to the `self-review` mechanism, and `:120`
stamps any `pending` row `genuine`. The check is behaving exactly as documented; the documented
semantics are what is wrong.

## Impact

- 70% of the check's genuine-signal volume (310/442) is a permanent false positive. A reviewer
  working the list correctly finds nothing actionable in it, which trains the whole check to be
  ignored.
- It distorts the corpus matrix: `self-review` shows 267 pending against 231 direct_fix, reading as
  a large unresolved self-review backlog that does not exist.
- The `build_pending_pile` sibling flag is unaffected (it scopes to `mechanism=build`), so the defect
  is specific to the assessment/summary sources being routed into a finding-resolution lifecycle they
  do not participate in.

## How to apply

- Separate the record kinds: an **assessment** (a decision) and a **scan summary** (a roll-up) are
  not findings and must not be scored on the finding-resolution axis. Either exclude them from the
  chain matrix or give them their own non-resolution bucket.
- Correct `checks/quality-chain.md:25` and `:120` in lock-step with the code — the doc currently
  legitimises the miscount.
- General rule: before stamping a state `genuine` because it is "unresolved", verify the record class
  CAN reach a resolved state. A status no producer ever writes makes the signal vacuous — the same
  archetype as [[2026-07-26-22-001]] (a branch no input reaches) and [[2026-07-26-22-002]] (a marker
  no producer emits).
