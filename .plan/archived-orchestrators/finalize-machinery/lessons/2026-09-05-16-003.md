# 2026-09-05-16-003 — mark-step-done accepts a --head-at-completion that resolves to no commit, so a fabricated anchor is persisted

## Context

Round 2 of pre-submission-self-review recorded `head_at_completion:
36a800cff8e9e0e01d5e2ba0d4b0c5f8b8e0c9a1` — not a commit (real SHA
`36a800cff22f322e5ee02b80da5ee02b80da5ee5d334c2b6b9`); a short hash extended to 40
chars instead of resolved through git. Accepted and written. Surfaced one dispatch
later when round 3's helper refused (`since_ref_unresolvable`) — the downstream
fail-closed was right but fires after an envelope is spent, and the anchor meanwhile
names a nonexistent tree.

## Root cause

A field that is a git ref everywhere read is an opaque string where written.
Validation lands at every consumer instead of the single producer.

## Proposed action

Resolve with `git rev-parse --verify {sha}^{commit}` at the write site; reject with
structured `error: unresolvable_head_at_completion`. Consider accepting short SHAs and
storing the resolved full form (removes the invention pressure).

## Impact

One wasted self-review round (~985K tokens across firings); structural point: an agent
fabricated a plausible identifier and only a fail-closed consumer stopped it becoming
history.

Staged in PLAN-06 (anchors-and-mutex, deliverable 1). Corpus original RETAINED until
PLAN-06 ships.
