# no-incident-references family 1 fires on the provenance citations its own exemption posture says it does not

## Observation

`_analyze_incident_reference_in_docs.py`'s "Exemption posture" section states:

> The genuinely-referential contexts that do exist (bot data-sheets, the rule provenance/catalog docs)
> fall outside the narration family above, so none needs a permanent exemption.

That claim is **false for family 1**. `_PLAN_MARSHALL_REF_RE = re.compile(r'plan-marshall#\d+', re.IGNORECASE)`
fires **unconditionally** — unlike families 4 and 5, it requires no accompanying incident noun. So a bot
data-sheet citing `plan-marshall#NNNN` as evidence provenance trips the rule, which is precisely the
context the posture section names as not needing an exemption.

Observed on plan `required-reviewer-returns-empty-list`: 5 findings in
`marketplace/bundles/plan-marshall/skills/automatic-review/standards/cuioss-review-bot.md`
(lines 500, 531, 579, 615, 617), every one an evidence-provenance citation where the mechanism was
already stated in full and the PR was named only as *where* it was observed — the analyzer's own
docstring lists that shape under "Deliberately NOT flagged".

## Why it matters

The rule ships unconditional on a premise its own family-1 pattern contradicts. The gap is not that the
rule is wrong to fire — a back-ticked reference is the sanctioned exempt form and is a genuine
improvement — but that the **documentation tells an author the opposite of what the code does**. An
author reading the posture section concludes a data-sheet citation is safe unquoted, writes it that way,
and is surprised by a quality-gate failure at finalize.

There is a second, sharper edge: the mechanical way to silence the rule is to drop `plan-marshall#1027`
to a bare `#1027`, which the pattern does not match. That defeats the detector while preserving exactly
what it objects to, and it is the move an author under time pressure will find first. The correct remedy
(back-ticking, which `_exemption_offset` tests via the inline-code span at the `#NNNN` offset) is
discoverable only by reading the analyzer source.

## Directive

Reconcile the analyzer's "Exemption posture" prose with family 1's actual unconditional behaviour. Either:

- correct the posture text to state that bot data-sheets and provenance/catalog docs DO fire on family 1
  and must use the back-ticked form, naming that form explicitly so the remedy is discoverable from the
  documentation rather than only from the source; or
- gate family 1 on an incident noun the way families 4 and 5 are gated, so the prose becomes true.

Do not close this by adding a per-file `plugin-doctor-disable` anywhere — `ext-triage-plugin`'s
"fix structurally, no suppression" rule governs.
