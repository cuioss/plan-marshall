## What was observed

Gate forwarded cluster count 1 where 6 distinct failing notations existed at
evaluation time (14 error lines over 7 notations whole-run). Root cause located in
instruction text: "the `bundle:skill:script` token in the line" is ambiguous — every
line carries TWO such tokens (emitting context, identical; failing script behind
`notation=`). Taking the first collapses all lines to one, reproducing the observed 1
exactly.

## Fix

Bucket by the `notation=` value, never by "the token in the line". One clause at the
authoring site.

## Why it matters

The gate is a SKIP decision: at all-zero signals lessons-capture never dispatches. A
counter one step from zero silently discards the entire script-failure lesson class.
