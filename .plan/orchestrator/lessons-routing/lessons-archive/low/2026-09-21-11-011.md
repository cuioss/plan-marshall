## Rule

Bot suggestions that expand scope beyond the tasked deliverables should be
declined with explicit intent linkage (covering TASK, tests, docs, ADR
settlement) rather than applied. Triage pattern: check TASK intent and ADR
settlement before accepting cross-cutting bot refactors — a suggestion can
be correct in isolation and still contradict what the plan was asked to do.

## Provenance

Promoted from process-compliance inbox `plan-07-opencode-repairs-006.md`:
both coderabbit inline suggestions on PR #1554 triaged taken_into_account
with Declined dispositions (wiring format vs pure-rendering intent;
sentinel reservation vs ADR-015 scope), review_body carried no independent
defect.
