# A re-fired finalize step emits no [DISPATCH] line, so the dispatch audit is blind where it matters

## Context

The `check-dispatch-audit` aspect on plan `shipped-guards-assume-the-meta-projects-own-layout` reported:

```
channel_completeness:
  dispatch_line_count: 8              # population: finalize_dispatcher_caller
  all_caller_dispatch_line_count: 29  # population: every_caller_in_work_log
  completion_count: 57
  dispatched_step_count: 7
  ratio: 0.14
  confidence: low
```

Against that, `work/metrics-dispatch-boundaries-6-finalize.toon` holds **24** recorded dispatch terminations. The finalize dispatcher emitted 8 `[DISPATCH]` lines for 24 dispatches — the channel under-records finalize dispatch by roughly 3x.

The cause is visible in the step ledger: `pre-submission-self-review` has `firing_count: 15`, `project:finalize-step-plugin-doctor` and `pre-push-quality-gate` have 6 each, `automatic-review` 6, `finalize-step-simplify` 4, `lessons-housekeeping` 4. A step's **first** dispatch emits a `[DISPATCH]` line; its re-fires do not.

## Root cause

`[DISPATCH]` emission is attached to entering a step, not to issuing a dispatch. When the finalize FOR loop re-enters from the top after a HEAD advance and re-fires every head-dependent step, those re-fires issue real dispatches that spend real tokens and record real boundary rows, but add no line to the channel the audit measures.

The failure mode is self-concealing in the worst direction. `channel_completeness` responds to a sparse channel by lowering its **own** `confidence` — so the audit correctly reports that it cannot see well, but it reports this most strongly on exactly the plans that re-fire most, which are the expensive plans the audit exists to explain. A cheap linear plan gets a trustworthy audit; a 9.6M-token plan with 15 firings of one step gets `confidence: low`.

Note also that `shape_violation` reported `0 violations` over a population of 29 here. That is not corroboration of dispatch discipline — both of its surfaces come from the same seam, so a re-fire that emits neither a resolve record nor a dispatch line cancels out and never becomes a violation.

## Proposed action

Emit a `[DISPATCH]` line on **every** dispatch issued, including a re-fire, and carry the firing ordinal on the line (e.g. `firing=7/15`) so the channel is reconcilable against `firing_count` and against the dispatch-boundary row count.

Then make `channel_completeness` compare `dispatch_line_count` against the **dispatch-boundary row count** for the phase rather than against `completion_count`; the boundary ledger is the independent second evidence source and is the right denominator for a channel that counts dispatches.

## Impact

Any consumer of the `[DISPATCH]` channel — the dispatch audit, cost attribution by role, and any future per-dispatch spend cap — sees roughly one third of finalize dispatch on a heavily re-firing plan, and is told about it only as a lowered confidence rather than as a count it can correct.

## Evidence

- aspect: execution_context_dispatch_audit — `channel_completeness.ratio: 0.14`, `confidence: low`, `dispatch_line_count: 8`
- `work/metrics-dispatch-boundaries-6-finalize.toon` — 24 rows
- `status.metadata.phase_steps["6-finalize"]` — `firing_count` 15 / 6 / 6 / 6 / 4 / 4 across six steps
- aspect: execution_context_dispatch_audit — `by_role` shows `phase-6-finalize` resolves=9, dispatch_lines=9, delta=0, so the two surfaces agree while both under-count

## Recurrence — 2026-09-13 (the-foreign-gate-population-and-branch-f-recovery)

Reproduced on a second plan, with the predicted self-concealment visible again:

```text
channel_completeness:
  dispatch_line_count: 10             # population: finalize_dispatcher_caller
  all_caller_dispatch_line_count: 24  # population: every_caller_in_work_log
  completion_count: 42
  dispatched_step_count: 7
  ratio: 0.238
  confidence: low
```

`work/metrics-dispatch-boundaries-6-finalize.toon` holds **28** recorded dispatch terminations
against those 10 lines — the same ~3x under-record. The re-firing steps are again the cause:
`ci-verify` and `automatic-review` at `firing_count: 6`, `pre-submission-self-review` at 5, and
`pre-push-quality-gate` / `plugin-doctor` / `lessons-housekeeping` at 4 each.

Two observations this recurrence adds:

- **`dispatch_coverage` degrades in the same direction.** 8 of 16 terminal finalize steps came back
  `no_evidence` — including every step that never dispatches at all (`push`, `branch-cleanup`,
  `ci-verify`, the deploy/sync/era-stamp project steps). Half the finalize surface is unclassifiable
  by this audit, and the report reads `missing_dispatch_emission: 0` over it, which is true and
  uninformative.
- **The original's warning about `shape_violation` held exactly.** It reported `0 violations` over a
  population of 24, with `by_role` deltas all zero — both surfaces agreeing while both under-count.
  A reader taking that zero as corroboration of dispatch discipline would be wrong for the second
  time on the second plan.

The proposed remedy is unchanged and still the right one: emit a line per dispatch *issued*
(including re-fires, carrying the firing ordinal), and compare against the dispatch-boundary row
count rather than `completion_count`. Both denominators already exist in the plan directory.
