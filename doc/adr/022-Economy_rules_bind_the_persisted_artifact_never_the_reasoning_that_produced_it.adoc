== Status

Proposed

== Context

Proposals to reduce what this system costs to run arrive regularly, and they
arrive aimed at generation: instruct the model to think less, reason more
tersely, deliberate within a word ceiling. The intuition is sound in the setting
those proposals are usually measured in — a short prompt against a long
response, where generation genuinely dominates the bill.

This system's cost has the opposite shape. A large context is assembled once and
re-read on every subsequent turn, so the dominant term is resident context
multiplied by turns, and generation is a small minority of billing weight even
after output's price premium is applied.

The discriminator between the two settings is the **input:output ratio of the
workload a result was measured on**:

[cols="1,2,2", options="header"]
|===
| | Where such results are measured | This system

| input per call
| a short prompt — a record and a few lines of instruction
| a large context, cached and resident

| output per call
| a long reasoning trace
| comparatively little

| dominant term
| *output*
| *input* — resident context × turns
|===

Short-prompt, single-shot work is one of the few regimes where generation
genuinely dominates a bill, and it is the regime nearly every published
token-saving result comes from. Almost none comes from long-lived agent
contexts. That alone makes generation a poor target here. But the structural
reason the proposals misfire is sharper than the arithmetic, and it survives any
re-measurement:

**The dispatch topology already discards reasoning.** A dispatched
`execution-context` leaf may deliberate at whatever length its effort level
warrants and returns a bounded struct; its reasoning does not cross the boundary
and the parent never pays to re-read it. The boundary is already the compressor
that a thinking-word-ceiling would be trying to install by instruction. There is
no reasoning-side lever left to pull, and the one knob that genuinely governs
deliberation depth — the effort level pinned per dispatch variant — is a
separate mechanism whose reduction this project rejects on measured grounds:
lowering it reduces examination, and the findings that stop appearing were being
found.

What does cross the boundary, and what is therefore paid for repeatedly, is the
**persisted artifact** — the return struct, the status field, the finding
record, the report body, the specification. Each such artifact enters context
once and is re-read for the remainder of every run that touches it, and each is
read by a party that did not produce it.

That second property is the one that inverts the advice. The reader of an
artifact has no access to the reasoning that justified it: the reasoning was
discarded at the boundary the artifact crossed. An artifact compressed to its
verdict is therefore not a compact record of a derivation — it is an assertion,
indistinguishable to its reader from a guess. Applying a brevity rule there
manufactures the vacuous-authority shape this project already spends effort
detecting, and it does so in the name of an economy the artifact surface does
not even dominate.

So there are two surfaces, with opposite economics and opposite disciplines, and
proposals routinely address the wrong one because both are loosely called
"output".

== Decision

**Brevity and economy rules attach to what persists across a boundary. They
never attach to the model's reasoning.** Two obligations follow.

**1. Do not regulate reasoning.** No standard, persona, or skill instructs an
agent to think less, reason more briefly, or deliberate within a length bound.
Such a rule is unenforceable — reasoning is not inspectable by a test, a
structural linter, or CI, so compliance can never be observed — and it targets
the surface the dispatch boundary already discards. Deliberation depth is
governed solely by the effort level of the dispatched variant, which is a
capability decision, not an economy one.

**2. Govern persisted artifacts by density, not brevity.** Every artifact that
crosses a boundary is bounded, and the bound has a fixed order:

* **The completeness floor comes first.** What the artifact's reader must have
  in order to decide — what failed and why, what was skipped and under what
  condition, what landed only partially, and the enumeration behind any claim of
  a set, a count, or a completeness — is never trimmed. Brevity is never the
  justification for dropping one of these.
* **The ceiling applies only to what the floor has released.** What is cut is
  restatement: narration of work already concluded, reasoning re-told after its
  conclusion is stated, and any copy of content a queryable sink already holds
  verbatim. The canonical move is to replace a restatement with a pointer at its
  source, which reduces size and raises trustworthiness together.

The discrimination is **restatement versus evidence**, and it is the whole rule;
the floor and the ceiling are its two halves. A shorter artifact that dropped a
failure is not a compact artifact, it is a wrong one — and the copy that
restates is also the copy that goes stale.

Because the rule binds the artifact rather than the agent, it is enforceable: an
artifact's shape can be checked structurally, and the surfaces that carry hard
bounds already are.

**The regime test screens incoming proposals.** A published economy result is
assessed by establishing the input:output ratio of the workload it was measured
on *before* asking whether it transfers. A result from an output-dominated
regime does not carry to an input-dominated one, and the two are not
distinguished by anything in the result's own framing.

== Consequences

=== Positive

* Cost proposals get routed correctly on first reading. A lever aimed at
  reasoning is rejected structurally rather than re-argued from measurement each
  time it arrives.
* The artifact discipline that does pay is stated once as a principle, so the
  standards that implement it per direction are recognisable as instances rather
  than independent conventions.
* Density and trustworthiness stop being in tension. Cutting restatement makes
  artifacts both smaller and more reliable, so the economy goal no longer
  competes with the anti-vacuity goal.
* An artifact's bound is machine-checkable, which the reasoning-side version
  never could be. Enforcement is available where the obligation is.

=== Negative

* Artifacts are larger than a pure brevity rule would make them, because every
  derivation they assert must be carried rather than summarised away.
* Authors must judge restatement against evidence per artifact. That judgement
  is domain work and cannot be applied by a sweep.
* The rule declines a lever that is genuinely effective elsewhere, so it will
  keep being proposed by anyone reasoning from results measured in an
  output-dominated regime.

=== Risks

* **"Density" becomes a licence to expand.** An author can justify any length by
  calling it evidence. Mitigation: the test is whether a later reader could
  reach the same conclusion without the bytes — content a sink already holds is
  restatement regardless of how load-bearing it feels.
* **The floor is applied to the ceiling's surface.** Carrying a full derivation
  into a field that exists as a routing summary defeats the field's purpose.
  Mitigation: hard-bounded fields keep their bounds; the derivation belongs in
  the sink the summary points at, not in the summary.
* **The boundary is assumed rather than checked.** The rule's premise is that
  reasoning does not cross a dispatch boundary. A surface that persists
  reasoning — a transcript, a verbose log, a report that quotes deliberation —
  puts it back on the paid side and inherits the artifact discipline, not the
  exemption.
* **The instrument understates the surface this rule declines to regulate.** The
  recorded billing-weighted total sums `input + output + round(0.1 ×
  cache_read) + round(1.25 × cache_creation)`, weighting output at 1× while every
  current model prices it at 5× input, so generation's share of real spend is
  understated fivefold in a figure classed as a derived cost and rendered as a
  first-class billing column. The correction does not reverse this decision —
  generation remains a minority term, and the decision rests on the boundary
  argument rather than on the share — but any re-argument that quotes the
  recorded figure will understate the surface it is arguing about. A related trap
  attends any such percentage triple: it reads either as token shares or as
  weighted-cost shares, and the two differ by more than an order of magnitude.
  The discriminator is internal — dividing the context terms by their weights
  must reproduce the independently measured average re-read factor. **Read the
  producing formula before interpreting the percentage.**

== Alternatives Considered

**Instruct agents to reason tersely.** Add a minimalism rule for thinking to the
foundational persona. Rejected on three independent grounds: it is
unenforceable, because reasoning is not inspectable by any check this system
has; it targets a surface the dispatch boundary already discards; and its
mechanism reduces to examining less, which is rejected on measurement rather
than weighed. The foundational persona is also the most expensive place in the
corpus to add prose, since it loads unconditionally into every dispatch.

**Apply one brevity rule to everything the model emits.** Treat reasoning and
persisted artifacts as a single "output" surface with a single length
discipline. Rejected: the two surfaces have opposite economics and opposite
readers. A rule short enough to matter on the discarded surface destroys the
derivation on the surface that is read by a party who cannot reconstruct it.

**Lower effort levels.** Reduce deliberation depth directly, through the knob
that actually controls it. Rejected on first-party measurement: the reduction in
spend is accompanied by findings ceasing to appear, and the phases that were not
lowered moved the other way — the prior quiet was under-examination, not
absence. This is a capability decision, and it is settled separately from
economy.

**Leave it to per-surface convention.** Let each standard decide its own bound
as it is written. Rejected: that is the state the rule replaces, and it is why
proposals aimed at reasoning keep being evaluated on their own terms. Without a
stated parent, the next surface re-derives the answer, and the next proposal
gets re-argued.

The rejected options share one property: each treats "how much the model emits"
as a single quantity with a single cost, when it is two quantities — one
discarded at a boundary and effectively unpaid, the other persisted and paid for
on every subsequent read.
