# Count and exclusivity words in Javadoc go stale when a caller or writer is added

Javadoc that states counts or exclusivity ("two callers", "the only writer") goes stale the moment a change adds a caller or writer, and nothing fails. Name the role instead, or list the callers in one place the caller-adding change has to touch. A change that adds a caller or writer greps the callee's and the field's Javadoc for count and exclusivity words before closing.

PLAN-V02-45: TASK-15 made `TerminalDispatch.retireFailedRelayConnection` a third caller of `ConnectionRetirement.retireConnectionAfterResponse` and `recordRetirement` a second writer of `retiredConnections`; the Javadoc kept "two callers" and the single-writer claim, costing one more review round.
