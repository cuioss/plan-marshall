title: "pr_intent_section truncates the PR Intent section mid-sentence, and the author cannot re-render it"
content: "## Problem

`pr_intent_section render` clips the distilled `## Intent` section at a fixed character budget
without regard to sentence or clause boundaries, so an over-budget draft lands in the PR body
truncated **mid-sentence**.

Observed on PR #1411 (plan `apply-the-cloud-plan-lane-contract-amendments`, 2026-09-04): the draft
was 1577 characters against a 1500-character budget, and the rendered section ends at 1496 with the
words *\"It does not alter which reviewers\"* — a sentence fragment. The clipped tail was the rest of
the non-goals list (which reviewers are required on any plan, that merge-gate conditions are
unchanged beyond verdict meaning, and that the change does not extend to other lanes). For a change
that touches the merge gate, that tail is precisely the blast-radius reassurance a reviewer needs.

## Why the author cannot work around it

The three properties compose into a trap:

1. The workflow forbids the authoring step from measuring or re-cutting its own draft — sizing is
   the renderer's job.
2. The renderer **appends**, so re-invoking it to produce a shorter section yields a *second*
   `## Intent` heading rather than replacing the first.
3. `ci pr view` does not return the body, so hand-repairing the section means reconstructing the
   whole PR body from scratch.

So the author is told not to size the draft, cannot re-render it, and cannot cheaply repair the
result. The only clean route left is a full `ci pr edit` rewrite of the body — disproportionate to a
renderer defect, and a per-PR workaround for something that will recur on every over-budget draft.

## Directive

Truncate at a **boundary**, not at a byte offset. When the draft exceeds the budget, cut at the last
sentence end that fits and append the visible-truncation marker the section already documents, so
the rendered text always ends as a complete sentence.

Two smaller improvements worth taking with it:

- **Report the overflow to the caller.** The render return should carry the draft length, the budget,
  and whether a clip occurred, so the step can log a specific WARNING instead of the author noticing
  a fragment by eye.
- **State the budget where the draft is authored.** The authoring guidance says not to measure the
  draft; it can still say what the ceiling is, so an author writing a long non-goals list knows it
  will be cut and can order the content accordingly.

## Evidence

- PR #1411 body, `## Intent` section: ends `\"It does not alter which reviewers\"` at 1496 chars.
- Draft retained at `.plan/temp/pr-intent-draft.md`: 1577 chars, complete.
- Renderer: `plan-marshall:phase-6-finalize:pr_intent_section render`, budget 1500."
